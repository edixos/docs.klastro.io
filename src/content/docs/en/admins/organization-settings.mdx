The **Settings** page holds your organization's profile and the details printed
on your invoices. Company details are optional: a school, a community or a
personal workspace can leave them empty.

## Before you start

- You need the **Admin** role to edit the settings. Admins find **Settings** in
  the sidebar.
- Other members can open **Organization Settings** from their user menu. They see
  the page with a **Read only** badge and the message **Only organisation admins
  can edit these settings. Contact your admin to make changes.**

## Edit the organization profile

<Steps>

1. In the sidebar, open **Settings**.

2. Update the fields you need (see the reference below).

3. Click **Save changes**. A **Changes saved** message confirms the update.

</Steps>

### Organisation profile

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Organisation name** | Between 2 and 100 characters. |
| **Organisation slug** | The identifier in your workspace URL. It cannot be changed after creation. |
| **Country** | Decides which company identifiers the page asks for. |
| **Legal company name** | Optional. |
| **SIREN** | France only. The 9-digit French company identifier. Once saved, it is permanent and cannot be changed. |
| **VAT number** | Optional. Shown for organizations outside France: the EU VAT identification number for EU companies. |

:::caution
Check the **SIREN** before you save it. Once saved, it is locked and the field
reads **Permanently assigned — cannot be changed.**
:::

### Company address

**Street address**, **City** and **Postal code** of your registered address,
used for invoicing and compliance.

### Billing information

Used on invoices. Leave these fields blank to use your company address.

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Billing email** | Invoices and receipts are sent here. |
| **Billing address**, **City**, **Postal code** | The address printed on invoices. |
| **Billing country** | The country of the billing address. |

## Company details at creation

When you create an organization, the **Create your Organization** form asks for
an **Organization name**, a **Workspace URL** and a **Country**. Company details
are optional: tick **This organization is a company** to add a **SIRET** (the
14-digit French establishment number), a **Billing address** and a **VAT
number**. Leave the box unticked for a school, community or personal workspace.
You can complete the details later from **Settings**.

## Switch organization

If you belong to several organizations, you switch between them without signing
out.

<Steps>

1. In the sidebar, click the organization switcher, which shows the current
   organization's name.

2. Under **Switch Organization**, pick another organization. With more than five
   organizations, use the search field to find it.

</Steps>

A **Switching to …** screen appears briefly, then the app shows the chosen
organization. Your role, seat and credits are those of that organization: each
membership is separate.

To choose from the full list instead, click **Exit Organization** in the same
menu. You land on the **Select Organization** page, where you can also create a
**New Organization**.

## Delete the organization

:::danger
Deleting an organization permanently removes all its members, sessions,
scenarios and billing data. This cannot be undone.
:::

<Steps>

1. In **Settings**, scroll to **Danger zone**.

2. Click **Delete organisation**.

3. Type the organization's slug to confirm, then click **Delete organisation**.

</Steps>
