The **Audit Log** is a chronological record of the security-relevant actions taken
in your organization, most recent first.

## Access

Only members with the **Admin** role can read the audit log. Open **Audit Log**
in the sidebar.

## Columns

| Column | Content |
|---|---|
| **When** | Date and time of the event. |
| **Event** | What happened, for example **Member added**. |
| **Actor** | The email of the person who did it. **System** marks an action Klastro took automatically, such as pausing a lab that ran out of credits. **Klastro** marks an action taken by the Klastro team. |
| **Target** | What the event is about (**Member**, **Session**, **Voucher**…), with a name or email when one is known. |
| **IP address** | Where the request came from. Shown on wide screens. |

Click the arrow at the end of a row (**Show details**) to see the event's full
details, and again (**Hide details**) to fold them.

The log shows 50 events per page.

## Filter events

| Filter | Use |
|---|---|
| **Category** | Keep one group of events (see below). **All categories** shows everything. |
| **Actor** | Keep the events of one person. Enter their **exact email**. |
| **From** / **To** | Keep events within a date range. **From** must be before **To**. |

Click **Filter** to apply, and **Clear** to remove every filter. When nothing
matches, the page reads **No event matches**.

## Events by category

### Members

| Event | Recorded when |
|---|---|
| **Member added** | An admin adds someone to the organization. |
| **Member role updated** | A member's role changes. |
| **Member removed** | A member is removed. |
| **Member invitation sent** | An invitation email goes to a new member. |
| **Member invitation resent** | An admin resends it. |
| **Member invitation revoked** | An invitation stops working, for example because the member was removed. |
| **Member invitation redeemed** / **Member invitation accepted** | The new member uses their invitation. |

### Organization

**Organization created**, **Organization settings updated** and **Organization
deleted** have no category of their own: they appear under **All categories**.

### Candidates

**Candidate invitation sent**, **Candidate invitation resent**, **Candidate
invitation revoked**, **Candidate invitation accepted**. See
[Send candidate invitations](/en/recruiters/send-invitations/).

### Billing

| Event | Recorded when |
|---|---|
| **Subscription activated** | A paid subscription starts. |
| **Subscription updated** | The subscription changes. |
| **Subscription canceled** | The subscription is canceled. |
| **Invoice paid** | An invoice is paid. |
| **Payment failed** | A payment fails. |
| **Seats purchased** / **Seats removed** | Paid seats are added to or removed from the subscription. |
| **Seat assigned** / **Seat released** | A paid seat is given to or taken back from a member. |
| **Credits transferred** | A member gives credits to another. |
| **Seat reservation requested** | Someone asks to enable pay-as-you-go seat reservation. |
| **Auto-purchase configured**, **Quota updated** | Organization billing settings change. |

### Vouchers

**Voucher redeemed**: a member redeemed a credit voucher.

### Sessions

| Event | Recorded when |
|---|---|
| **Session created** | A lab starts. |
| **Session paused** / **Session resumed** | A lab is paused or resumed. |
| **Session terminated** | A lab ends. |
| **Session auto-paused (out of credits)** | A lab was paused because its owner ran out of credits. |

### Assessments

**Assessment created**, **Assessment updated**, **Assessment deleted**,
**Assessment started**, **Assessment terminated**, **Assessment duration
adjusted**.

### Studio

**Scenario draft created**, **Scenario draft deleted**, **Scenario published**.

### Tracks

**Track created**, **Track updated**, **Track archived**, **Track material
uploaded**, **Track material deleted**.

### Training

**Training created**, **Training updated**, **Training launched**, **Training
ended**, **Training canceled**, **Trainees enrolled**, **Trainees invited**,
**Trainee removed**, **Connected in to a trainee's session**, **Training
environment recreated**, **Training environment stopped**. See
[Run a cohort](/en/instructors/cohorts/).

### Platform

Actions the Klastro team took on your organization:

- **Commercial mode changed by a platform admin**
- **Session limit updated by a platform admin**
- **Subscription auto-canceled by the platform**
- **Voucher generated by a platform admin**, **Voucher revoked by a platform
  admin**, **Voucher emailed by a platform admin**, **Voucher deleted by a
  platform admin**

:::note
Platform events always appear, so you can see when the Klastro team acted on your
organization. Their **Actor** reads **Klastro**, and the staff member's identity,
IP address and internal details are not shown.
:::
