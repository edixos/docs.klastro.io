The **Team** page is where an admin decides who belongs to the organization and
what each person can do. A member's **role** says what they may manage; their
**seat** says which scenarios they reach and which credits they receive.

## Before you start

- You need the **Admin** role. The **Team** page appears in the sidebar for
  admins only.
- To give paid seats, your organization needs a Pro or Instructor subscription.
  See [Subscribe to a plan](/en/billing/subscribe/).

## Roles

| Role | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Admin** | Full control over the organization, its members and billing. |
| **Billing Admin** | Manages subscriptions and buys seats for members. |
| **Learner** | Self-paced labs, playgrounds and tutorials. |
| **Instructor** | Authors scenarios and runs cohorts, with an Instructor seat. |
| **Recruiter** | Manages assessments and candidate invitations. |

Which roles you can choose depends on how your organization is billed:

- a **self-serve subscription** offers **Learner**, **Instructor**, **Admin**
  and **Billing Admin**;
- an **Enterprise contract** also offers **Recruiter**, because hiring
  assessments are part of the contract.

## Permissions

| | Admin | Billing Admin | Learner | Instructor | Recruiter |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Open the **Team** page: add, remove and change roles | Yes | No | No | No | No |
| Edit **Settings** | Yes | Read only | Read only | Read only | Read only |
| Read the **Audit Log** | Yes | No | No | No | No |
| **Billing**: **Plan**, **Seats** and **History** tabs | Yes | Yes | No | No | No |
| **Billing**: **Overview** and **Usage** of their own credits | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Hold a paid seat | Yes | No | Yes | Yes | No |
| Give their own credits to a member | Yes | No | No | With an Instructor seat | No |
| Redeem a credit voucher | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |

What a member can **launch** depends on their seat, not their role. Scenario
Studio and cohorts need an **Instructor** seat; Pro scenarios need a **Pro
Learner** or **Instructor** seat. See [Plans and credits](/en/billing/plans-and-credits/#seats).

## Add a member

<Steps>

1. In the sidebar, open **Team**, then click **Add member**.

2. In the **Add team member** dialog, enter the person's **Email address** and
   pick a **Role**.

3. Click **Add member**.

</Steps>

The person is added straight away and a **Member added** confirmation appears.
They sign in with email, Google or GitHub.

- If the person has no Klastro account yet, one is created for them and they
  receive an invitation email to finish setting it up. Until they do, the
  **Status** column shows **Invited**, then **Setup started** once they have
  opened the link.
- If they already have an account, they find the organization the next time
  they sign in.

:::note
During the private preview, you may see **This person has no Klastro account
yet.** New accounts then need a platform invitation before you can add the
person.
:::

Every new member starts on the free **Trainee** seat with **no credits**. Being
added never costs you anything. To let them run labs, give them a paid seat or
some credits (see below).

### Resend an invitation

If the invitation was not used, click **Resend invitation** in the member's
**Status** column. A new invitation link is sent to the member's address.

- **Link expired** means the previous link timed out: resend it.
- **Email not delivered** means the last email bounced. Check the address, then
  resend.
- After a resend, the button shows **Resend available in …s** until you can send
  again.

## Change a member's role

Pick the new role in the **Role** column of the member's row. The change applies
at once and a **Role updated** confirmation appears.

Changing a role never changes the member's seat. A member who held a paid seat
keeps it until you release it.

## Give a member a seat

In the **Seat** column, choose **Pro Learner** or **Instructor**, then confirm
with **Allocate seat**. The seat comes out of the pool you already paid for, so
your bill does not change.

To take a seat back, choose **Trainee** and confirm with **Release seat**. The
member keeps their account but only reaches free scenarios and the sessions an
instructor assigns them.

A member can receive a seat only after they have signed in; until then the column
reads **Awaiting sign-in**. For the full procedure, see
[Manage seats](/en/billing/seats/).

## Give a member credits

You can hand some of **your own** credits to another member, for example so a
learner without a paid seat can run labs.

<Steps>

1. On the member's row, click the coins icon (**Give credits**).

2. Enter the **Amount**. The dialog shows how much is **available to give**.
   Decimals are allowed.

3. Click **Give credits**.

</Steps>

The credits leave your balance and are added to theirs, to spend on their own
labs. Credits that came with your seat stay with the seat and cannot be given,
so the amount you can give may be lower than what you can spend.

## Remove a member

<Steps>

1. On the member's row, click the remove icon (**Remove member**).

2. In the **Remove team member** dialog, click **Remove member**.

</Steps>

:::caution
The member immediately loses access to all of the organization's resources, and
any invitation link they were sent stops working. This cannot be undone: to bring
them back, add them again.
:::

:::tip
If the member holds a paid seat, release it first so it goes back to your pool
with its credits.
:::

## What each member sees

Klastro scopes what members see to themselves:

- the **Billing** page shows each member their **own** credits and their own
  labs only. Admins and billing admins also see the organization's plan, seats
  and credit history;
- the **Team** page lists each member's **Credits** balance, which only their
  own labs draw down. It can be negative when a running lab outlasted the
  credits that started it.

Organizations under an Enterprise contract do not have the **Seat** and
**Credits** columns: their capacity is set by contract.
