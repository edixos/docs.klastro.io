A candidate's report is produced when their assessment ends, whether they finished it or time ran out.
This page describes each of its sections.

**Access:** **Recruiter** or admin in the organization.

## Open a report

| From | Action |
|---|---|
| **Assess** → **Results** | Eye icon (**View report**) on the candidate's row. |
| **Assess** → **Invitations** → an invitation | **View report** button, shown once the assessment is over. |
| **Assess** → **Assessments** | The score in the **Final Score** column (**View report**), or the **View report** button on the run page. |

If the report shows **Report unavailable**, the assessment is most likely still in progress. The
**Back to invitation** link returns to the invitation page.

## Header

| Element | Content |
|---|---|
| Title | Candidate's name (or their email if there is none). |
| Subtitle | Assessment name · candidate's email. |
| Result badge | **Passed** if the score reaches the template's passing score, **Failed** otherwise. |
| Verdict badge | The recorded verdict, if any (see [Record a verdict](/en/recruiters/verdicts/)). |

## Headline figures

| Figure | Value | Secondary line |
|---|---|---|
| **Score** | Percentage achieved, with a gauge. | Points earned / points available (`12 / 20 pts`). |
| **Passing mark** | The template's passing score, in %. | "threshold to pass" |
| **Duration** | Time the candidate spent. | "hands-on time" |
| **Tasks passed** | Tasks passed / total tasks, across all scenarios. | Number of scenarios. |

Percentages are coloured in three fixed bands: green from 75%, amber from 50 to 74%, red below 50%.

:::note
The colours do not depend on your template's passing score. A 72% shown in amber can well be **Passed**
if your threshold is 70%. Rely on the **Passed** / **Failed** badge.
:::

## Assessment record

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **Result** | **Passed** or **Failed**. |
| **Score** | Points earned / points available. |
| **Score %** | Percentage achieved. |
| **Status** | `COMPLETED`, `TIMED_OUT` (time ran out) or `REVIEWED` (a verdict was recorded). |
| **Verdict** | The recorded verdict, or "—". |
| **Started** | Start date and time. |
| **Completed** | End date and time. |
| **Duration** | Total duration of the run. |
| **Assessment** | Template name. |

## Scenario breakdown

One row per scenario in the assessment, with:

- the scenario name;
- tasks passed out of the total (`3/4 tasks`);
- time spent on the scenario;
- points earned (`12/20 pts`);
- a gauge and the scenario's percentage.

## Skill radar

The radar shows the percentage achieved in each skill area the scenarios assess. Hover over a point to
see the percentage and the points behind it (`80% (8/10 pts)`).

The radar shows at most **8** skills: those carrying the most points. Any others are listed under the
chart, with their points and percentage.

The radar does not appear if the assessment measured no skills.

## Strengths and areas to improve

Next to the radar:

- **Strengths** lists the skills where the candidate scored **75% or more**;
- **Areas to improve** lists those where they scored **below 50%**.

A skill between 50 and 74% appears in neither list.

## Task details

One collapsible panel per scenario. Its header repeats the scenario's percentage, tasks passed and
points. Expanded, each task shows:

| Element | Content |
|---|---|
| Icon | Green tick if the task passed, red cross otherwise. |
| Identifier | The task's identifier within the scenario. |
| Points | Points earned / points available. |
| Attempts | How many times the candidate submitted the task (`2 attempts`). |
| Checks | The validation rules of the **last** attempt: description, pass or fail, and how long the check took in milliseconds. A rule marked **(optional)** was not required to pass the task. |

This detail tells you **what** the candidate got right or missed, not just how much. To understand how
tasks are checked on the candidate's side, see [Tasks and checks](/en/lab/tasks-and-checks/).

## Candidate notes

If the candidate used their scratchpad during the lab, the **Candidate notes** section reproduces its
content as written (see [Lab notes](/en/lab/notes/)). The section does not appear if the scratchpad is
empty.

:::tip
Notes often show the candidate's reasoning: hypotheses, abandoned leads, prepared commands. Read them
before you give your verdict.
:::

## Recruiter review

Once a verdict is recorded, the **Recruiter review** section shows the **Verdict** and, if any, the
notes of the person who recorded it.
