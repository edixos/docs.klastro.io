An **assessment template** is the reusable definition you send to candidates: a sequence of graded
scenarios, a single timer that covers all of them, and a passing score. Every invitation points to a
template; every candidate who starts produces a separate **assessment run**.

**Prerequisite:** be a **Recruiter** or an admin in an organization on a business contract.

## Create a template

<Steps>

1. Open **Assess**, then **Templates**, and click **New template**.

   If your organization has Scenario Studio and you can author in it, a menu offers two choices:

   - **Compose from the catalog** opens the template editor described below;
   - **Write a challenge in Studio**: author a custom scenario first. Once published, it appears
     in the template catalog.

   Otherwise the button opens the editor directly.

2. Fill in **Assessment details**:

   | Field | Rule |
   |---|---|
   | **Name** | Required, 120 characters at most. |
   | **Description** | Optional, 500 characters at most. Tell the candidate what to expect. |
   | **Total duration (min)** | Required, from 1 to 480 minutes (8 hours). |
   | **Passing score (%)** | Required, from 0 to 100. Defaults to 70. |

3. In the **Scenario catalog** on the right, find the scenarios to include. You can narrow it by:

   - search (name, description or tag) with **Search scenarios…**;
   - provenance: **Official** (Klastro scenarios) or **Studio** (written in your organization);
   - difficulty: **Beginner**, **Intermediate**, **Advanced**;
   - tag, by clicking one.

   Only graded challenges appear in this catalog.

4. Click **Add** on each scenario you want. The button turns into **Added** and the scenario joins
   the **Scenario order** list.

5. Set the order (see below), then click **Create assessment**.

</Steps>

The new template appears under **All assessment templates** with the **Draft** status.

## Order the scenarios

Under **Scenario order**, drag a row by its handle (**Drag to reorder**) to move it. This is the order
in which the candidate sees the scenarios.

To take a scenario out, click **Remove scenario** on its row.

:::note
In their lobby, the candidate can complete the scenarios in any order they prefer. The template order
is the order of presentation, not an enforced path.
:::

## Set the duration

Until you change it, **Total duration (min)** follows the scenarios: the sum of their estimated
durations plus 25% headroom, rounded up to the next 5 minutes. The text under the field says so
("Follows the scenarios: … estimated, plus 25% headroom").

If you type another value, the **Use the suggested duration** link brings back the automatic figure.

The **Time allocation** bar shows each scenario's share of the total time. If it turns red, the
scenario durations exceed the total assessment time.

:::caution
A template whose scenarios exceed the total duration does not save correctly. Increase the total
duration or remove a scenario before you click **Create assessment** or **Update assessment**.
:::

## Check with the preview

Click **Preview** to see the assessment as the candidate will: name, description, total duration, and
the scenario list with each scenario's task count. Scoring details and passing thresholds are hidden in
this view, just as they are for the candidate.

## Edit a template

<Steps>

1. Open **Assess**, then **Templates**.

2. Click the template under **All assessment templates**.

3. Change the details, or add, remove or reorder scenarios.

4. Click **Update assessment**. The "Assessment updated" message confirms it was saved.

</Steps>

## Read the template list

| Column | Content |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Template name. |
| **Scenarios** | Number of scenarios. |
| **Duration** | Total duration. |
| **Pass %** | Passing score. |
| **Status** | **Published**, **Draft** or **Archived**. |
| **Created** | Creation date. |

:::note
The invitation form offers every template that is not archived, drafts included. A draft shows its
status next to its name there.
:::
