The score measures what the candidate did in the lab; the **verdict** is your hiring decision. Klastro
keeps both side by side: the verdict shows in the report list and on the report itself, with your notes.

**Prerequisite:** be a **Recruiter** or an admin in an organization on a business contract.

## The four verdicts

| Verdict | Shown in **Results** and on the report |
|---|---|
| Strong hire | **STRONG HIRE** |
| Hire | **HIRE** |
| No hire | **NO HIRE** |
| Strong no hire | **STRONG NO HIRE** |

The first two show in green, the last two in red.

## Record a verdict

The verdict is recorded through the Klastro [MCP integration](/en/mcp/overview/), that is, from an AI
assistant connected to your account. The report page displays the verdict but has no form to enter it.

<Steps>

1. Check that the candidate's assessment is over: their report must be listed under **Assess** →
   **Results**. A run still in progress cannot receive a verdict.

2. Read the report (see [Read a candidate report](/en/recruiters/candidate-report/)).

3. In your assistant connected to Klastro, ask it to record the verdict, naming the candidate, for
   example: "Record a *Hire* verdict on Jane Smith's report, with the note: fast diagnosis, sound method,
   needs work on networking." The assistant uses the `submit_report_review` tool.

4. Reload the report in Klastro.

</Steps>

The verdict badge appears at the top of the report, the **Recruiter review** section shows the verdict
and your notes, and the report status changes to **Reviewed**.

- Notes are optional and limited to 5,000 characters.
- To change your mind, record a new verdict: it replaces the previous one, notes included.

:::caution
The verdict is visible to every recruiter and admin in the organization. Write your notes as a document
the candidate might one day ask to see.
:::

:::note
The score is a measurement, not a decision. The hiring decision is yours, made with human involvement:
that is exactly what the verdict is for.
:::

## Filter the report list

<Steps>

1. Open **Assess**, then **Results**. The **Candidate Reports** page lists the completed assessments of
   the whole organization.

2. In the **Filters** row, choose a **Status**:

   - **All statuses**;
   - **Completed**: the candidate went to the end, no verdict yet;
   - **Timed out**: the allotted time ran out, no verdict yet;
   - **Reviewed**: a verdict has been recorded.

3. Optionally choose an **Assessment** to keep a single template.

4. To clear everything, click **Clear filters**.

</Steps>

:::tip
Filter on **Completed** and **Timed out** to get the candidates still waiting for your verdict.
:::

The **All reports** table shows 10 reports per page, with these columns:

| Column | Content |
|---|---|
| **Candidate** | Name and email. |
| **Assessment** | Template name. |
| **Score** | Percentage, points (`18/25 pts`) and gauge. |
| **Result** | **Passed** or **Failed**. |
| **Status** | **Completed**, **Timed out** or **Reviewed**. |
| **Verdict** | The verdict, or "—". |
| **Duration** | Time between the start and the end of the run. |
| **Completed** | End date. |

On a narrow screen some columns are hidden; **Candidate**, **Score** and the **View report** icon always
stay visible.

The counters at the top of the page (**Completed**, **Passed**, **Failed**) cover the reports shown on
the table's current page.
