In this tutorial you build an assessment template from the scenario catalog, invite a candidate,
watch their run live, then read their report. Plan on about ten minutes of your own time, plus
however long the candidate spends in the lab.

Before you start, you need:

- an account in an organization on a **business contract**: assessments, templates, invitations
  and reports are part of the Enterprise plan (see [Plans and credits](/en/billing/plans-and-credits/));
- the **Recruiter** role or an admin role in that organization
  (see [Members and roles](/en/admins/members-roles/));
- a candidate's email address. For a first try, a second address of your own works well.

:::tip
If the pages under **Assess** show "Assessments require a business contract", your organization is
on a self-serve subscription. Contact Klastro to enable the Enterprise plan.
:::

## 1. Build the template

<Steps>

1. In the sidebar, open **Assess**, then **Templates**.

2. Click **New template**. If a menu offers two choices, pick **Compose from the catalog**.

3. Under **Assessment details**, enter a **Name**, for example
   "Kubernetes troubleshooting — Senior SRE". Leave **Passing score (%)** at 70.

4. In the **Scenario catalog** on the right, click **Add** on two scenarios. They appear on the
   left under **Scenario order**, and **Total duration (min)** fills itself in from their
   estimated durations.

5. Click **Preview** to see the assessment as the candidate will, then close the preview.

6. Click **Create assessment**. The "Assessment created" message confirms it was saved.

</Steps>

## 2. Invite the candidate

<Steps>

1. Open **Assess**, then **Invitations**, and click **Invite candidate**.

2. Fill in the candidate's **Full name** and **Email address**.

3. Under **Template**, choose the template you just created.

4. Under **Expiry date**, click **7 days**.

5. Leave **Auto approval** selected so the candidate can start without waiting for you.

6. Click **Send invitation**. The "Invitation sent" message appears and the candidate receives an
   email with a link to their invitation.

</Steps>

The candidate then accepts the invitation and starts the assessment
(see [Accept an invitation](/en/learners/accept-invitation/)).

## 3. Watch the run live

<Steps>

1. Once the candidate has started, open **Assess**, then **Assessments**. Their run appears under
   **All assessments**.

2. Click the row to open the run. The **Live** badge means the page updates by itself, and the
   **Time Remaining** card shows the countdown.

3. Under **Sessions**, click a scenario to open its supervision panel. You see the candidate's
   terminal in read-only mode, marked **Observing**.

</Steps>

You cannot type in that terminal: you watch, the candidate works. Scores stay hidden until the
assessment is over.

## 4. Read the report

<Steps>

1. When the candidate has finished (or time has run out), open **Assess**, then **Results**.

2. On the candidate's row, click the eye icon (**View report**).

3. Go through the report: **Score**, **Passing mark**, **Scenario breakdown**, **Skill radar** and
   **Task details**.

</Steps>

The **Passed** or **Failed** badge in the top-right corner compares the candidate's score with the
template's passing score.

## 5. Record your verdict

The verdict (**STRONG HIRE**, **HIRE**, **NO HIRE**, **STRONG NO HIRE**) is recorded through the
Klastro MCP integration, then shows up under **Results** and on the report. The steps are in
[Record a verdict](/en/recruiters/verdicts/).
