An invitation gives **one** person, identified by their email address, the right to take **one**
assessment template before a deadline. The candidate can only accept it and start while signed in with
that address.

**Prerequisites:**

- be a **Recruiter** or an admin in an organization on a business contract;
- have at least one assessment template (see [Create and edit assessment templates](/en/recruiters/assessment-templates/)).

## Send an invitation

<Steps>

1. Open **Assess**, then **Invitations**, and click **Invite candidate**.

2. Under **Candidate**, fill in:

   - **Full name**: 2 to 100 characters;
   - **Email address**: the address the candidate will sign in with.

3. Under **Assessment**, open **Template** (**Search and select an assessment**), type a few letters to
   filter, then pick the template. Archived templates are not offered.

4. Set the **Expiry date**: click **7 days**, **14 days** or **30 days**, or pick an exact date and
   time. The date must be in the future.

5. Under **Session settings**, choose the authorization mode:

   - **Auto approval** (the default): the candidate starts immediately;
   - **Manual approval**: you confirm before the session starts.

6. Turn on **Require webcam** if you want to tell the candidate the assessment is webcam-proctored
   (see [Webcam and proctoring](/en/recruiters/proctoring-supervision/)).

7. Click **Send invitation**.

</Steps>

The "Invitation sent — Sent to …" message confirms it. The candidate immediately receives an email with
a link to their invitation and how long they have. The email differs depending on whether the candidate
already has a Klastro account. What they see next is described in
[Accept an invitation](/en/learners/accept-invitation/).

**Reset** clears the form without sending anything.

:::caution[Manual approval]
With **Manual approval**, a candidate who starts lands on the "Waiting for Authorization" screen, and
their scenarios are only prepared once you approve. The dashboard has no button to approve: approval is
given through the Klastro [MCP integration](/en/mcp/overview/). If you don't use that integration,
choose **Auto approval**.
:::

## Understand the statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Created** | The invitation exists and the email has gone out. The candidate has not responded yet. |
| **Sent** | You resent the email with **Resend email**. The candidate has not responded yet. |
| **Accepted** | The candidate accepted the invitation. They can start the assessment. |
| **Revoked** | You cancelled the invitation. The candidate can no longer accept it. |
| **Expired** | The expiry date passed without the invitation being used. |

Once the run has begun, the lifecycle card on the invitation page (**Track Status Lifecycle**) extends
this path with the assessment's state (**Active**, **Completed**, **Timed out**, **Reviewed**), then
**PASSED** or **FAILED**.

:::note
The status changes to **Expired** when someone tries to use the invitation after the deadline. Until
then, the **Expires** date shows in red on the invitation page.
:::

:::caution
The expiry still applies after acceptance: a candidate who accepted but did not start before the
expiry date can no longer start. Pick a window that leaves them time to book a slot.
:::

## Resend the email

Useful when the candidate cannot find the message.

<Steps>

1. Open **Assess**, then **Invitations**, and click the invitation's row.

2. Click **Resend email**.

</Steps>

The "Invitation email resent successfully" message confirms it, and the status changes to **Sent**. The
button only appears for a **Created** or **Sent** invitation. An invitation past its expiry date cannot
be resent: create a new one.

## Revoke an invitation

<Steps>

1. Open the invitation page and click **Revoke**.

2. In the **Revoke invitation?** window, click **Revoke invitation**.

</Steps>

:::danger
Revoking is permanent: the candidate can no longer accept this invitation. To invite them again, send
them a new invitation.
:::

You can only revoke a **Created** or **Sent** invitation. An **Accepted** invitation can no longer be
revoked.

## Find an invitation

The **Invitations** page shows three counters (**Pending**, **Accepted**, **Expired**) and the
**All invitations** table, 10 rows per page.

To filter the table, use:

- **Status**: one of the five statuses above, or **All statuses**;
- **Assessment**: a template, or **All assessments**;
- two dates bounding the creation date (**Created from date**, **Created to date**).

**Clear filters** removes them all.

An invitation page groups:

| Card | Content |
|---|---|
| **Candidate** | Name and email. |
| **Assessment** | Template name and ID. |
| **Settings** | **Authorization mode** (**Automatic** or **Manual approval**) and **Webcam required**. |
| **Timeline** | **Created** and **Expires**. |
| **Track Status Lifecycle** | The path described above. |
| **Assessment Result** | Once the candidate has started: status, score, passing score, result, dates and duration. |

When the assessment is over, the **View report** button opens the candidate's report
(see [Read a candidate report](/en/recruiters/candidate-report/)).
