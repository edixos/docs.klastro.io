Each problem below is laid out as **Symptom**, **Cause** and **Fix**.

## I forgot my password

**Symptom.** You cannot sign in because you no longer know your password, or you never finished choosing one.

**Cause.** Passwords are not stored in a way anyone can read back, so they can only be reset.

**Fix.**

1. Go to [app.klastro.io/auth/recovery](https://app.klastro.io/auth/recovery). The page is titled **Access your account**.
2. Enter your **Email address** and select **Send code**.
3. Open the email from Klastro, copy the **Verification code** and select **Verify & continue**.
4. Follow the prompts to choose a new password.

If no email arrives within a few minutes, check your spam folder and that you typed the address your account uses.

## "Sign in failed"

**Symptom.** The sign-in page shows **Sign in failed** with a message.

**Cause.** Usually a wrong email or password, or an account that was created with a social sign-in rather than a password.

**Fix.** Check the email address and password. If you originally signed in with one of the **Continue with …** buttons shown under **Or continue with**, use the same button again. If you still cannot sign in, reset your password as described above.

## "Session expired" or "Flow expired"

**Symptom.** You see **Session expired** ("Your session has expired. Please sign in again to continue.") or **Flow expired** ("This authentication flow is no longer valid…").

**Cause.** You stayed too long on a sign-in page, used an old link, or your session timed out.

**Fix.** Select **Sign in again** and start over. Do not reuse old sign-in or recovery links from your browser history.

## "Security check failed"

**Symptom.** You see **Security check failed**, or **The security check did not complete. Refresh the page to try again.**

**Cause.** You navigated back with the browser's back button during sign-in, or a content blocker stopped the security check from loading (**The security check could not load. Turn off any content blocker for this page, then refresh.**).

**Fix.** Refresh the page and start again without using the back button. If the check cannot load, allow app.klastro.io in your ad or content blocker, then refresh.

## "Registration requires an invitation"

**Symptom.** You try to create an account and see **Klastro is in private preview. Registration requires an invitation.**

**Cause.** During the private preview, new accounts can only be created from an invitation link.

**Fix.** Use the link in your invitation email. If you were invited to an assessment or by your organization, see [Invitations](/en/troubleshooting/invitations/). Otherwise, ask the person or organization who should invite you.

## "Register with the email address this invitation was sent to"

**Symptom.** Registration is refused with this message.

**Cause.** An invitation is tied to one email address.

**Fix.** Register with the exact address the invitation email was sent to. If you need to use a different address, ask for a new invitation to that address.

## I'm asked to accept the terms

**Symptom.** After signing in, a **Terms of Use and Privacy Policy** screen blocks the app.

**Cause.** You must accept the current Terms of Use and Privacy Policy before using Klastro, the first time and whenever they change.

**Fix.** Read the documents, then select **Accept and continue**. If you see **Your acceptance could not be recorded**, try again: it is safe to repeat.

## I don't see my organization, or I see the wrong one

**Symptom.** After signing in you land on **Select Organization**, or the app shows sessions, invitations or tickets from another organization than the one you expect.

**Cause.** Everything in Klastro (labs, credits, assessments, tickets) belongs to an organization. If you belong to several, you work in one at a time.

**Fix.**

- On **Select Organization**, choose the organization you want. Use **Search organizations...** if the list is long.
- Once in the app, open the organization menu at the top of the left sidebar and select **Switch Organization**.
- If the organization you expect is missing, you have not been added to it yet. Ask one of its admins to invite you; see [Members and roles](/en/admins/members-roles/).

As a candidate, the organization that invited you appears under **Your Organizations** once you have an invitation. If you see **No Invitations Yet**, check your email for the invitation link.

## "Unable to load your organizations"

**Symptom.** **Select Organization** shows **Unable to load your organizations. Please try again.**

**Cause.** A temporary network or service problem.

**Fix.** Refresh the page. If it keeps happening, sign out with **Log out**, sign in again, and if it persists [open a support ticket](/en/support/open-ticket/) with the category **Account & access**.

## "Account issue"

**Symptom.** You see **Account issue** ("There is an issue with your account. Please contact support.").

**Cause.** Something on your account needs a person to look at it.

**Fix.** Select **Contact support**. If you cannot reach the Help Desk because you cannot sign in, ask an admin of your organization to open a ticket for you.
