Klastro sends two kinds of invitation by email:

- an **account invitation**, to join Klastro or an organization (**Join your team on Klastro**, **Your invitation to Klastro**);
- an **assessment invitation**, to take a technical assessment as a candidate.

Each problem below is laid out as **Symptom**, **Cause** and **Fix**. For the normal flow, see [Accept an invitation](/en/learners/accept-invitation/).

## Account invitations

### "This invitation has expired"

**Symptom.** The link opens a page titled **This invitation has expired**.

**Cause.** Invitation links stop working after a few days.

**Fix.** Ask the person who invited you for a new one. An organization admin can send it again from the team page with **Resend invitation**; the new email contains a working link.

### "This invitation was already used"

**Symptom.** The page shows **This invitation was already used** ("The account for this invitation is already set up."), or **This link was already used**.

**Cause.** Each link works once. Your account already exists, or you opened the link before and did not finish choosing a password.

**Fix.** Select **Sign in** and use your account. If you never finished setting your password, open [app.klastro.io/auth/recovery](https://app.klastro.io/auth/recovery) and follow [the recovery steps](/en/troubleshooting/sign-in/). You can also ask your organization admin to resend the invitation.

### "This invitation was withdrawn"

**Symptom.** The page shows **This invitation was withdrawn**.

**Cause.** The person who invited you cancelled it.

**Fix.** Contact them if you think this is a mistake. Only they can send you a new invitation.

### "A newer invitation was sent"

**Symptom.** The page shows **A newer invitation was sent**.

**Cause.** You were invited again, which replaced the earlier link.

**Fix.** Find the most recent invitation email and use its link.

### "This link does not work"

**Symptom.** The page shows **This link does not work** ("The invitation link is incomplete or not valid.").

**Cause.** The link was cut when it was copied, for example across two lines, or it was modified.

**Fix.** Open the link directly from the email instead of copying it. If that fails, ask for a new invitation.

### "We could not check this invitation"

**Symptom.** **We could not check this invitation. Check your connection and try again.**, or **We could not start account setup. Try again in a moment.**

**Cause.** A network problem or a temporary service issue.

**Fix.** Check your connection and select **Try again**.

## Assessment invitations (candidates)

### "Invitation Expired"

**Symptom.** The invitation shows **Invitation Expired**, or the list shows **This invitation has expired. Contact the organization for a new one.**

**Cause.** The recruiter set an expiry date and it has passed. The invitation's **Expires On** date shows when.

**Fix.** Contact the recruiter or organization that invited you and ask for a new invitation. Klastro cannot extend it for you. When an invitation shows **Expiring Soon**, start the assessment before that date.

### "Invitation Revoked"

**Symptom.** The invitation shows **Invitation Revoked** ("This invitation has been revoked by the organization.").

**Cause.** The organization cancelled it.

**Fix.** Contact the organization if you think this is a mistake.

### "Unable to load invitation"

**Symptom.** **Unable to load invitation**: "This invitation may have been revoked, expired, or is for a different email address."

**Cause.** You are signed in with a different email address from the one the invitation was sent to, or the invitation is no longer valid.

**Fix.** Sign out and sign in with the address that received the invitation email. Also check that the right organization is selected; see [Sign-in and organizations](/en/troubleshooting/sign-in/).

### I can't start the assessment

| What you see | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| **Accept Invitation First** | You have not accepted the invitation yet. | Go back to **Your Invitations** and select **Accept**. |
| **Too many sessions are running right now. Please try again in a few minutes.** | The platform or the organization is at capacity. | Wait a few minutes and select **Start Assessment** again. Your invitation is not affected. |
| **Failed to start assessment** | A temporary problem. | Try again. If it persists, [report it](/en/support/open-ticket/) with the category **Assessment**. |
| **Assessment Completed** | You already finished this assessment. | Nothing to do: the recruiter receives your results. |

If the assessment needs a recruiter's approval before it starts, the lab shows **Waiting for your session to be authorized…** until they approve it. See [Lab preparation](/en/troubleshooting/provisioning/).

## For recruiters and admins

| Situation | Fix |
|---|---|
| A candidate's invitation expired. | Send a new invitation from **Invitations**. See [Send invitations](/en/recruiters/send-invitations/). |
| **Already invited**: "A pending invitation exists for this email. Revoke it first." | Open the existing invitation, select **Revoke**, then invite again. Or select **Resend email** to send the same invitation again. |
| A member's invitation shows **Link expired** on the team page. | Select **Resend invitation**: the new email revives it. |
| A member's invitation shows **Setup started**. | They opened the link but did not choose a password. Ask them to open [app.klastro.io/auth/recovery](https://app.klastro.io/auth/recovery) to recover their account, or resend the invitation. |
