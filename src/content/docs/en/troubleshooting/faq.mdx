## Account and access

**I forgot my password. How do I get back in?**
Go to [app.klastro.io/auth/recovery](https://app.klastro.io/auth/recovery), enter your email and use the code you receive. See [Sign-in and organizations](/en/troubleshooting/sign-in/).

**Why can't I create an account?**
Klastro is in private preview: you need an invitation link. See [Sign-in and organizations](/en/troubleshooting/sign-in/).

**I don't see the right organization.**
Open the organization menu at the top of the sidebar and select **Switch Organization**. See [Sign-in and organizations](/en/troubleshooting/sign-in/).

## Invitations

**My invitation link has expired. What now?**
Ask the person or organization who invited you for a new one. See [Invitation problems](/en/troubleshooting/invitations/).

**The link says it was already used.**
Your account already exists: sign in, or use **Forgot your password?**. See [Invitation problems](/en/troubleshooting/invitations/).

## Labs

**My lab is taking a long time to start.**
Labs with several machines take longer. Wait while the step counter moves. See [Lab preparation](/en/troubleshooting/provisioning/).

**The terminal says "Terminal disconnected".**
Select **Reconnect**: your environment kept running. See [Terminal problems](/en/troubleshooting/terminal/).

**My check fails, but I think my work is right.**
Check names, namespaces and readiness, then **Check again**. See [Checks and validation](/en/troubleshooting/checks/).

**Do I lose my work if I leave the lab?**
Not if you pause it: **Pause & resume later** keeps your environment. Ending a session destroys it for good. See [Manage your sessions](/en/learners/manage-sessions/).

## Credits and limits

**Why was my lab paused?**
Your credits ran out, or, in a class, your instructor moved you to another step. See [Credits and paused labs](/en/troubleshooting/credits-and-pauses/).

**Why can't I start another lab?**
You may be at your plan's session limit, short of credits, or your organization may be at capacity. See [Credits and paused labs](/en/troubleshooting/credits-and-pauses/).

**Does a paused lab cost credits?**
No. Paused time is not charged. See [Plans and credits](/en/billing/plans-and-credits/).

## Assessments

**My assessment requires a webcam. How do I prepare?**
Allow the camera for app.klastro.io in your browser and system before starting. See [Webcam and camera permissions](/en/troubleshooting/webcam/).

**Can I pause the assessment timer?**
No. Once started, the timer cannot be paused, even while you report a problem. See [Assessment mode](/en/lab/assessment-mode/).

## AI assistants

**Can I use Klastro from Claude, Cursor or VS Code?**
Yes, through the Klastro MCP server. See [Klastro and AI assistants](/en/mcp/overview/) and [Connect your AI client](/en/mcp/connect-clients/).

## Getting help

**How do I contact support?**
Open a ticket from **Help Desk** in the sidebar, or select **Report a problem** inside a lab. See [Open a support ticket](/en/support/open-ticket/) and [Report a problem from a lab](/en/support/report-lab-problem/).

**Is there a community forum?**
No. For questions and problems, open a support ticket: replies arrive by email.
