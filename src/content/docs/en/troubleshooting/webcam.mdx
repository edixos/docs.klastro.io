:::note
In the current version, Klastro does not turn on or capture your camera during an assessment. The webcam requirement is information from the recruiter: your browser should not ask for camera access. If the recruiter wants to see you on video, use your own video-call tool as they instruct.
:::

The invitation may show under **Webcam** that it is **Required** or **Optional**. This is information to read: follow the recruiter's instructions. For what proctoring means for you, see [Assessment mode](/en/lab/assessment-mode/).

:::caution
Once started, the assessment timer cannot be paused. If the recruiter asks you to test your camera or video tool, do it **before** you select **Start Assessment**.
:::

## If the recruiter asks you to use a video tool

The sections below only apply in that case. Camera access is controlled by your **browser** and your **operating system**, not by Klastro: check both for your video-call tool.

### The browser blocks the camera

**Symptom.** The camera does not work on the video-call site, the address bar shows a crossed-out camera icon, or the browser never asks for permission.

**Cause.** You previously refused camera access for this site, or the browser's default setting blocks cameras.

**Fix.** Allow the camera for the video-call site in your browser, then reload the page.

<Tabs syncKey="browser">
<TabItem label="Chrome">

1. On the video-call site, select the icon to the left of the address in the address bar (**View site information**).
2. Set **Camera** to **Allow**. If you do not see it, select **Site settings** and set **Camera** to **Allow** there.
3. Reload the page.

To check the global setting, open `chrome://settings/content/camera`: the video-call site must not be listed under the sites not allowed to use your camera.

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Firefox">

1. On the video-call site, select the permissions icon to the left of the address in the address bar.
2. Next to the camera permission marked **Blocked** (or **Blocked Temporarily**), select the **×** to clear it.
3. Reload the page and accept the camera request when Firefox asks.

To check the global setting, open **Settings** > **Privacy & Security** > **Permissions** > **Camera** > **Settings…** and make sure the video-call site is not set to **Block**.

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Safari">

1. With the video-call site open, choose **Safari** > **Settings…** in the menu bar (or **Settings for the video-call site…**).
2. Open the **Websites** tab, then **Camera** in the sidebar.
3. Set the video-call site to **Allow** (or **Ask**).
4. Reload the page.

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Edge">

1. On the video-call site, select the lock icon to the left of the address in the address bar.
2. Select **Permissions for this site** and set **Camera** to **Allow**.
3. Reload the page.

To check the global setting, open `edge://settings/content/camera`.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

### The operating system blocks the camera

**Symptom.** The browser allows the camera but no image comes through, or the browser says the camera is unavailable.

**Cause.** The operating system does not let your browser use the camera.

**Fix.**

- **macOS**: open **System Settings** > **Privacy & Security** > **Camera** and turn on your browser. Quit and reopen the browser afterwards.
- **Windows 11**: open **Settings** > **Privacy & security** > **Camera** and turn on **Camera access**, **Let apps access your camera** and **Let desktop apps access your camera**.

### Another application is using the camera

**Symptom.** The camera is allowed everywhere but still does not start, or shows a black image.

**Cause.** Another application (a video-call tool, another browser tab) is holding the camera. Some computers only let one application use it at a time.

**Fix.** Close video-call applications and other tabs that use the camera, then reload the video-call site. On a laptop with a physical camera cover or a camera key, make sure the camera is enabled.

### A company-managed computer

**Symptom.** The camera settings are greyed out or reset themselves.

**Cause.** Your organization's IT policy controls camera access.

**Fix.** Use a personal computer for the assessment, or ask your IT team to allow the camera for the video-call site.

## Still not working

If the recruiter asks for video and you cannot make it work, contact them **before** starting. If a problem appears during the assessment, select **Report a problem** in the lab's top bar; see [Report a problem from a lab](/en/support/report-lab-problem/).
