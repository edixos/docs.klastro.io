In this tutorial you build a small challenge: the learner has to scale a Deployment called
`web` from one replica to three. You place the environment, a setup step that deploys `web`,
a graded task with its check, then try the whole thing in a preview before publishing it.
Allow about twenty minutes, a few of which are spent waiting for the preview to provision.

Before you start, you need:

- an organization whose plan includes Scenario Studio (the Instructor plan, or an enterprise
  contract — see [Plans and credits](/en/billing/plans-and-credits/));
- in a subscription organization, an **Instructor seat** assigned to your account
  (see [Seats](/en/billing/seats/)). In a contract organization, the instructor, recruiter or
  admin role is enough (see [Members and roles](/en/admins/members-roles/)).

:::tip[Previews are free]
Everything you launch from the Studio to test costs nothing: previews debit no credits, and test
runs of your checks are never recorded against a learner.
:::

## 1. Create the draft

<Steps>

1. In the sidebar, open **Studio**, then **Scenario Studio**.

2. Click **New draft**.

3. In **Name**, type "Scale a Deployment".

4. In **Product type**, keep **Challenge — graded, hints cost points**.

5. In **Start from**, keep **An empty canvas**, then click **Create draft**.

   The empty canvas opens. Everything you do on it is saved automatically: the chip in the
   toolbar moves from **Unsaved changes** to **Saved**.

</Steps>

## 2. Place the environment

<Steps>

1. In the palette, under **Anchor**, click **Environment**. The node appears on the canvas.
   (You can also drag it where you want it.)

2. Click the **Environment** node to open its form.

3. In **Environment type**, pick an environment that contains a Kubernetes cluster, for example
   **Virtual Cluster** if your platform offers it. Below the list, chips summarize what the
   environment can do (number of machines, in-browser editor, pause and resume…).

4. Leave **Requests** and **Limits** empty: the environment applies its own values. Click **Done**.

</Steps>

## 3. Add the setup step

The setup step prepares what the learner finds on arrival: here, a `web` Deployment with a
single replica.

<Steps>

1. In the palette, under **Setup steps**, click **Manifests**.

2. Wire the environment to this step: drag from the output handle of the **Environment** node to
   the step's node. The pipeline always starts at the environment.

3. Click the step's node. In **Name**, type `deploy-web`. The name must be unique within the
   scenario.

4. Under **Manifests**, click **Add resource**, then choose **Deployment**.

5. Fill in the form:
   - **Name**: `web`
   - **Namespace**: `default`
   - **Image**: `nginx:1.27`
   - **Replicas**: `1`

6. Click **Done**.

</Steps>

## 4. Add the task and its check

<Steps>

1. In the palette, under **Grading**, click **Task**.

2. Wire the `deploy-web` step to the task. A task is wired from the step whose result it checks.

3. Click the task's node and fill in:
   - **Task id**: `scale-web`
   - **Title**: "Scale web to three replicas"
   - **Instruction**: "The `web` Deployment in the `default` namespace runs a single replica.
     Bring it to three ready replicas."
   - **Points**: `10`
   - **Max attempts**: `3`

4. Under **Validation**, click **Add check**, then fill in:
   - **Description**: "Three replicas are ready"
   - **Type**: **KubernetesResource — one field of one resource**
   - **API version**: `apps/v1`
   - **Kind**: `Deployment`
   - **Name**: `web`
   - **Namespace**: `default`
   - **JSONPath**: `.status.readyReplicas`
   - **CEL expression**: `exists && value == "3"`
   - **Points**: `10`

   Keep **Required** ticked.

5. Click **Done**.

</Steps>

:::note
With a **JSONPath** set, the expression reads the field as text in `value`: compare it to `"3"`,
quotes included. The variables available for each check type are listed in
[Setup steps and checks](/en/instructors/studio-actions-checks/).
:::

## 5. Check the canvas

<Steps>

1. In the toolbar, click **Check**.

2. If "No blocking problems found." appears, carry on. Otherwise the affected nodes carry a badge:
   open them, and each node's **Issues** tab says what is missing.

</Steps>

## 6. Try the scenario in a preview

<Steps>

1. In the toolbar, click **Preview**. The preview drawer opens below the canvas.

2. Click **Launch preview**. "Provisioning the environment. The terminal appears once it is
   ready." shows while the environment is prepared. Meanwhile the `deploy-web` node moves from
   **Queued** to **Running**, then **Applied**.

3. When the terminal appears, look at the starting state:

   ```bash
   kubectl get deployment web -n default
   ```

4. On the task node, click **Test validation**. The check fails: there is still only one
   replica. That is the expected result.

5. Do the learner's work in the terminal:

   ```bash
   kubectl scale deployment web -n default --replicas=3
   kubectl rollout status deployment web -n default
   ```

6. Click **Test validation** again. The task's **Results** tab shows "1/1 checks · 10/10 pts".

</Steps>

A preview lasts one hour. **Extend** resets the countdown to one hour, at most three times.
When you are done, click **Stop** in the drawer header.

## 7. Publish

<Steps>

1. In the toolbar, click **Publish**.

2. In **Scenario name**, type `scale-a-deployment`. The name is lowercase and dash-separated, and
   it is fixed after the first publish.

3. Click **Publish**. "Published as scale-a-deployment." confirms it.

</Steps>

Your challenge now appears in your organization's catalog with the **Studio** badge. Members can
launch it like any other challenge, and you can add it to a track.
