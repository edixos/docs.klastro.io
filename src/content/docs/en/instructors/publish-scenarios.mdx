A **draft** is your working canvas in Scenario Studio. **Publish** compiles it into a scenario
that your organization's members find in their catalog. Nothing is published until you say so,
and the draft stays open for later versions.

Before you start, you need access to Scenario Studio
(see [Your first scenario](/en/instructors/studio-first-scenario/)).

## Choose the product type

The product type decides how the scenario is presented and launched. You pick it when creating
the draft (**Product type**) and can change it later in **Settings**.

| Type | What it is for | Tasks |
|---|---|---|
| **Tutorial — guided, free hints** | A guided, step-by-step lab. | At least one |
| **Challenge — graded, hints cost points** | A real, graded task. | At least one |
| **Playground — no tasks** | An open environment with no instructions and no grading. | None |
| **Assessment — exam material** | Exam material, run only inside a proctored Assessment. | At least one |

- Changing the type re-checks what your organization may author.
- The **Assessment** type is only available to organizations on an enterprise contract. An
  Assessment scenario is not launched from the catalog: it is added to an assessment template
  (see [Assessment templates](/en/recruiters/assessment-templates/)).
- A scenario that is not a Playground cannot be tested or published without at least one task:
  the **Add a task** banner shows above the canvas while one is missing.

## Set the scenario settings

Click **Settings** in the toolbar. This panel holds everything about the scenario that is not a
node on the canvas.

| Field | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Display name** | The scenario's title in the catalog. |
| **Description** | The scenario's introduction text. |
| **Tags** | The skills the scenario covers. The catalog can filter by tag. |
| **Product type** | See above. |
| **Difficulty** | **Beginner**, **Intermediate** or **Advanced**. |
| **Estimated duration** | A duration in seconds, e.g. `2700s`. It also feeds the price of a cohort place. |
| **Card image** | Optional. Empty derives one from the environment type. |
| **Cleanup TTL (seconds)** | How long a session lives before it is torn down. |
| **Passing score** | The score needed to pass. |

The points total is shown under the passing score: it is recomputed from the tasks on every
publish.

## Validate, then publish

<Steps>

1. In the toolbar, click **Check**. Fix every error reported: the affected node's **Issues** tab
   says what to do. Warnings (a task worth no points, a task wired to no step…) do not block
   publishing.

2. Test every task at least once in a preview
   (see [Setup steps and checks](/en/instructors/studio-actions-checks/)).

3. Click **Publish**.

4. On the first publish, enter a **Scenario name**: lowercase and dash-separated, for example
   `scale-a-deployment`.

5. Click **Publish**. "Published as …" confirms it.

</Steps>

:::caution[The name is permanent]
The **Scenario name** is fixed after the first publish, so a typo cannot fork a second scenario.
To publish under another name, create a new draft.
:::

The published scenario appears in your organization's catalog with the **Studio** badge. The
catalog's **All sources** filter lets you show only your organization's scenarios, or only
Klastro's **Official** ones. Scenarios published from the Studio are visible only inside your
organization.

## Edit a published scenario

A published scenario is edited from the draft that published it.

<Steps>

1. Open **Studio**, then **Scenario Studio**. The **Published as** column of the **Drafts** list
   shows which scenario each draft published.

2. Click the draft's name to reopen its canvas.

3. Make your changes, then test them in a preview.

4. Click **Publish**. The dialog is titled **Republish scenario** and the name can no longer be
   changed. Click **Republish**.

</Steps>

## Start from an existing scenario

You can start from one of your organization's scenarios or from an official Klastro scenario: it
is decompiled into a laid-out canvas that you are free to change.

- From the Studio: **New draft**, then search for the scenario in **Start from**. The list separates
  **Your organization's Studio** and **Official Klastro**.
- From the catalog: on a scenario's page, click **Clone to Draft**. The new-draft dialog opens,
  pre-filled with that scenario.

The new draft is not linked to any published scenario: on its first publish it asks for a new name
and creates a new scenario. The original scenario is left unchanged.

## Delete a draft

In the **Drafts** list, click the row's bin icon. A running preview is stopped on the way.

:::danger
Deletion is immediate, with no confirmation. A scenario already published from that draft stays
live, but you can no longer republish it: that would take a new draft, published under another
name.
:::

## Several people on one draft

The draft saves automatically. If someone else (or you, in another tab) edits it at the same time,
the banner "This draft changed elsewhere — your edits are no longer being saved." appears: click
**Reload** to pick up the current version before carrying on.

:::tip
Studio drafts can also be created, previewed and published from an AI assistant connected to
Klastro. See [AI integrations (MCP)](/en/mcp/overview/).
:::
