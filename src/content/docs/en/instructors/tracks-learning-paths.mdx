Klastro has two things with similar names:

- a **track** (**Train** menu) is a curriculum you compose: an ordered, reusable sequence of
  scenarios plus its materials. You then deliver it to a group as a **cohort**;
- a **certification path** (**Learn** menu) is a roadmap provided by Klastro, which each learner
  follows alone, at their own pace.

## Compose a track

Before you start, you need:

- in a subscription organization, an **Instructor seat** assigned to your account
  (see [Seats](/en/billing/seats/));
- in a contract organization, the instructor or admin role
  (see [Members and roles](/en/admins/members-roles/));
- published scenarios: Klastro's catalog, or your own
  (see [Publish and manage your scenarios](/en/instructors/publish-scenarios/)).

### Create the track

<Steps>

1. In the sidebar, open **Train**, then **Tracks**.

2. Click **New track**.

3. Enter a **Name**, for example "Kubernetes Fundamentals — 3 days", and optionally a
   **Description**: who it is for and what they leave knowing.

4. Click **Create draft**. The track's page opens in three steps: **Scenarios**, **Materials** and
   **Details**.

</Steps>

Everything you do from here saves automatically. The indicator at the top of the page goes
through **Saving…** then **Saved**; "Add a name to save" means a name is missing.

### Build the sequence

<Steps>

1. On the **Scenarios** step, click **Add a scenario…**.

2. Search for a scenario. The list separates **Your scenarios (Studio)** and the **Klastro catalog**.
   A scenario marked **Pro** can only be launched on a plan that reaches Pro scenarios.

3. Add the labs in the order trainees should meet them. Use the arrows to move a scenario earlier
   or later, and the cross to remove it.

4. Click **Continue to Materials**.

</Steps>

### Add materials

Materials go with the labs: slides, recordings, documentation. This step is optional.

<Steps>

1. On the **Materials** step, click **Add material**.

2. Choose the **Kind**: **Document**, **Video** or **Link**. It only changes what the trainee sees.

3. Under **Material**, choose **External link** and paste the URL, or **Uploaded file** and click
   **Choose a file…**. An uploaded file is 32 MB at most: link a video instead.

4. Enter a **Title** and, if needed, a **Description**, then click **Add**.

5. Click **Continue to Details** and check the name and description.

</Steps>

### Read the price of a place

The **Price per place** panel beside the track shows what one trainee costs once your plan's
included places are used up. Enter a **Cohort length** in minutes to simulate the price: a track
has no length of its own, each cohort sets one. The price depends on the scenarios' environments
and their estimated duration. The details are in [Create a cohort](/en/instructors/cohorts/). In a
contract organization no place is charged and this panel does not appear.

### Publish the track

Click **Publish** at the top of the page. The button stays inactive while the track has no name or
no scenario. Only a published track is offered when composing a cohort.

**Deliver as a cohort** takes you straight to creating a cohort
(see [Create a cohort](/en/instructors/cohorts/)).

:::note[Editing a published track never changes a running class]
A cohort copies the track's sequence and materials when it is composed. So you can improve a
published track at any time: only cohorts composed afterwards get the changes.
:::

### Archive a track

In the **Tracks** list, click the row's archive icon. An archived track is no longer offered when
composing a cohort, but it is not deleted: the cohorts that came from it stay intact.

## Point your learners to certification paths

Certification paths are roadmaps designed by Klastro, from the first concept to exam readiness
(Kubernetes, security, Linux). They are the same for every organization: you cannot create or edit
them.

What to know before recommending them:

- your learners find them under **Learn**, then **Certification Paths**, filterable by **Topic** and
  **Level**;
- each step points to a catalog scenario. A step whose scenario is not in your organization's
  catalog is marked **Unavailable**;
- progress is declared by the learner (**Mark complete**). They are milestones, not credentials,
  and locked steps recommend an order rather than enforce it;
- progress is personal and follows the learner across organizations. You do not see it from your
  account.

For a programme taught in class, with your own order and live monitoring, compose a track and
deliver it as a cohort instead. The learner's side is described in
[Catalog, challenges and paths](/en/learners/catalog-challenges-paths/).
