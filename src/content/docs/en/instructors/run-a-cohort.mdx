Once a cohort is launched, its page becomes your class monitor: who has arrived, where each person
is, which checks pass, who is asking for help. This page covers every action available during the
class and after it.

Before you start, you need a launched cohort (see [Create a cohort](/en/instructors/cohorts/)).
Open **Train**, then **Cohorts**: the cohort is listed under **Live now**.

## Read the roster

During the class, the **Live** indicator means the table updates on its own (**Reconnecting…** if
the connection drops). Each row is one trainee.

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Trainee** | Their name and email, or their help request if their hand is up. |
| **Status** | **Enrolled**, **In lab** or **Finished**. **Not started**: they have not opened their environment yet. |
| **Environment** | Their environment's state. A warning icon and "Stopped: …" flag an environment that failed or went away. |
| **Progress** | The step of the sequence they are on, e.g. "Step 2 of 4". |
| **Type** | The kind of scenario in progress: tutorial, challenge, playground. |
| **Earlier steps** | The score earned on each step they have already left. |
| **Checks** | The checks passed on the current step. |
| **Current task** | The task they are working on. |

Some columns are hidden on small screens. The actions always stay visible on the right.

## Move trainees through the sequence

The class does not move in lockstep: each trainee moves through the sequence individually.

<Steps>

1. To move a trainee to the next scenario, click **Advance** on their row. The button disappears
   once they are on the last step.

2. To send them to any step, forward or back, click the arrow to the left of their name. The
   **Their run through the class** panel lists every step.

3. On the step you want, click **Move here**. "Moved to step …" confirms the move.

</Steps>

Leaving a step does not destroy the lab: it is paused with the trainee's work on it, and the step
shows **kept**. Moving the trainee back to that step restarts that same environment, as they left
it. Their score on the step they leave is recorded at the moment they leave.

:::caution[The cohort's time is limited]
No new environment can be started once the cohort's length has elapsed: with less than five minutes
left, **Advance** and **Move here** are refused. End the class then, or schedule a new cohort to
carry on.
:::

## Fix a trainee's environment

In the **Their run through the class** panel, the current step offers three actions:

| Action | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Join** | Opens a terminal in the trainee's environment (see below). |
| **Restart** | Replaces the current step's environment with a fresh one, on the same step. With no environment, it creates one. |
| **Stop** | Stops this environment and leaves it stopped, without touching the rest of the class. |

When an environment has failed or gone away, a **Recreate the environment** icon also appears
directly on the trainee's row.

:::caution
**Restart** and **Stop** run without confirmation and destroy the current environment. The score
is recorded first, but the terminal history and anything not yet checked are lost.
:::

## Join a trainee's environment

<Steps>

1. On the trainee's row, or on their current step, click **Join**.

2. The **…'s environment** window opens with a terminal in their lab. You work in it just as they
   do.

</Steps>

The trainee is told so in their own lab, and the connection is recorded. You get the terminal,
never the passwords of their app views. **Join** is only offered during the class, once the
trainee has started their environment, and only to the instructor running the cohort.

## Answer raised hands

During the class, a trainee can click **Ask for help** in their lab and, if they want, describe
what has them stuck.

<Steps>

1. The roster header shows "… waiting", and a hand appears under the trainee's name with their
   message or the step concerned.

2. Go and help: beside them, or with **Join**.

3. Click **Mark as handled**. The hand disappears from their row.

</Steps>

The trainee can also withdraw the request themselves. Raised hands are addressed to you alone: for a
broken environment you cannot fix, open a support ticket instead.

## Follow scores step by step

The **Their run through the class** panel shows a trainee's whole run, during and after the class:

- the current step shows its checks live;
- each finished step shows its recorded score, and its list of checks expands;
- a step not reached yet is marked **not reached**.

Only challenges are scored: tutorials and playgrounds produce no score.

## Update the materials

The cohort's materials stay editable during and after the class, without touching the original
track.

<Steps>

1. In the cohort's **Materials** card, click **Edit**.

2. In **Session materials**, click **Add a material**, choose the **Type**, enter the **Title** and
   the **Link**.

3. Click **Save**. Participants find the materials in their training, even after it ends.

</Steps>

## End the class

<Steps>

1. Click **End session** at the top of the page.

2. Confirm. The class closes for everyone on the roster and their environments are stopped.

</Steps>

In a subscription organization, the credits charged at launch are not refunded: ending early costs
the same as running to the end.

## Read the results and feedback

Once the cohort has finished, its page shows two more cards:

- **Results**: each participant's score, step by step, and their total. They stay available after
  the session ends.
- **What the class said**: the average rating out of 5, the number of replies out of the roster
  size, the spread of ratings and the comments. Participants can give feedback while the class runs
  and after it has finished, and change it later.

:::note[Anonymous feedback]
Feedback is only signed when the participant ticked the box to put their name on it. Otherwise you
see the rating and comment marked **Anonymous**, with no way to tell who wrote them.
:::
