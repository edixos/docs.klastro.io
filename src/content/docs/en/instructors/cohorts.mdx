A **cohort** is one delivery of a track to a group at a scheduled time: an instructor-led class.
Every trainee on the roster gets their own lab environment, and you follow the class live from
the cohort's page (see [Run a cohort](/en/instructors/run-a-cohort/)).

Before you start, you need:

- in a subscription organization, an **Instructor seat** assigned to your account
  (see [Seats](/en/billing/seats/)); in a contract organization, the instructor or admin role
  (see [Members and roles](/en/admins/members-roles/));
- a **published** track containing at least one scenario
  (see [Compose a track](/en/instructors/tracks-learning-paths/)).

:::note[Places and seats are different things]
A **place** is one trainee's slot in one cohort. It is paid in credits, from your own balance,
when the cohort launches. A **seat** is a named subscription in the organization (Pro,
Instructor), managed from billing. Enrolling someone in a cohort uses no seat and does not change
the one they hold. See [Seats](/en/billing/seats/).
:::

## 1. Pick the track

<Steps>

1. In the sidebar, open **Train**, then **Cohorts**.

2. Click **New cohort**. The wizard shows three steps: **Track**, **Schedule** and **Roster**.

3. Under **Pick a track**, click the track to deliver. The cohort copies its sequence and
   materials: editing the track later will not change this class.

</Steps>

## 2. Schedule it

<Steps>

1. Under **When it runs**, enter a **Name**, for example "Kubernetes Fundamentals — September".
   The track's name is suggested by default.

2. Pick the **Scheduled start**. It is an announcement for participants: nothing provisions or
   charges off it. You launch the class yourself.

3. Set the **Length** in minutes. It is suggested from the scenarios' estimated durations. It is
   the wall-clock length of the whole class, not of one scenario: the sequence is worked through
   inside it, and it is the time each trainee's environment is given.

4. Click **Compose cohort**. The cohort is created as a draft: it costs nothing and already shows
   in the list.

</Steps>

## 3. Enrol participants

On the **Roster** step, the **Who is attending** panel offers two ways to add places.

<Steps>

1. For people who are already members of the organization: under **Enrol members**, tick them,
   then click **Enrol**. Enrolment sends no invitation. A member must have joined the organization
   to be enrolled.

2. For people who are not members yet: in **Invite by email**, enter the addresses, one per line or
   comma-separated, then click **Invite**. A repeated address counts once.

</Steps>

An invited address with no Klastro account yet receives an email invitation, and the person joins
your organization to attend the class. An address that already matches an account is added
directly, with no email: let the person know yourself. A cohort takes up to 500 participants.

To remove someone, open the cohort's page and click the remove icon on their row in the **Roster**
table. This is only possible while the cohort has not been launched.

:::tip
Missing some names? Click **Save and finish later**. From the cohort's page, **Resume composing**
takes you straight back to the roster.
:::

## 4. Read the price

The **What it will cost** panel updates with every roster change.

| Line | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Places on the roster** | The number of trainees enrolled. |
| **Included by your plan** | The places your plan covers at no extra cost, for each cohort. |
| **Extra places** | The places beyond that, with their unit price in credits. |
| **Total on launch** | What will be debited from your balance when you launch. |

The price of an extra place depends on the environments of the scenarios in the sequence and their
estimated duration, capped at the cohort's length. A longer cohort can therefore cost more. Plans
and included places are described in [Plans and credits](/en/billing/plans-and-credits/).

In a contract organization, the cohort is covered by the contract: no credits are debited and no
price is shown.

## 5. Launch the cohort

<Steps>

1. Click **Launch**. The button becomes active fifteen minutes before the **Scheduled start**;
   before that it reads "Opens in …".

2. If your class is ready early, click **Start now**. The confirmation reminds you that the
   cohort's length starts running from the launch.

3. Read the confirmation: it states the exact amount debited from your balance and the number of
   environments started. Click **Launch and pay …**, or **Launch** when the cohort is inside your
   plan's allowance.

</Steps>

:::caution[What becomes final at launch]
- Credits are debited up front, from **your** balance, and are not refunded if the class ends
  early.
- The roster is fixed: nobody can be added or removed.
- The cohort's length runs from the launch. Once it has elapsed, no new environment can be started
  for this class.
:::

After launch, each trainee starts their own environment from **My Training**: it is built at that
moment, so the session's time is spent on the work. Participants need no credits for their cohort
labs. What they see is described in [Join a training session](/en/learners/join-training/).

## Cancel a draft cohort

On the page of a cohort that has not been launched, click **Cancel this cohort**, then confirm. It
leaves your upcoming classes without being deleted: the roster and the sequence stay readable.
Nothing is refunded because nothing was charged.
