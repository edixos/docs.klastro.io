The **Notes** tab is a plain-text scratchpad inside the lab. Use it to keep
commands that worked, values you will need again, or your reasoning as you go.

**Before you start:** notes are available in tutorials, challenges and
assessments. A playground has no Notes tab.

## Write notes

<Steps>

1. In the left pane, click the **Notes** tab, next to **Instructions**.

2. Type or paste anything you like. The notepad is plain text in a monospaced
   font, so commands pasted from the terminal keep their exact spacing.

3. Watch the indicator at the top right of the pane:

   | Indicator | Meaning |
   |---|---|
   | **Autosaved** | Nothing new to save |
   | **Unsaved changes…** | You are typing; it saves when you pause |
   | **Saving…** | Your notes are being saved |
   | **Saved** | Your latest text is stored |
   | **Not saved — retrying on next edit** | The last save failed |

4. Click **Instructions** to go back to your tasks. Your notes, and any text not
   yet saved, stay in place while you switch.

</Steps>

Notes save about a second after you stop typing, and immediately when you click
outside the notepad.

:::caution
If you see **Not saved — retrying on next edit**, your latest text is only in
your browser. Type any character to trigger a new save, and wait for **Saved**
before you leave the lab or reload the page.
:::

## Where notes are kept

| Lab | Your notes belong to |
|---|---|
| A tutorial or challenge you started yourself | That one lab. Opening the same lab again later shows them; a new lab starts with an empty notepad. |
| An assessment | The whole assessment. The same notepad follows you through every scenario of it. |

Notes are stored with your account, not in the lab environment, so they are not
affected by anything you do in the terminal. A notepad holds up to 256 KB of text.

## Who can read your notes

| Lab | Who can read them |
|---|---|
| A tutorial or challenge you started yourself | Only you |
| An assessment | You, and the recruiter who reviews your assessment: your notes appear in your candidate report |

:::caution[Assessments: your notes are part of what the recruiter sees]
The notepad's hint text says notes are private, but in an assessment the
recruiter reads them alongside your results. Write them as you would want a
reviewer to see them — explaining your approach there can help them understand
your work. See [Read a candidate report](/en/recruiters/candidate-report/) for the
recruiter's side.
:::
