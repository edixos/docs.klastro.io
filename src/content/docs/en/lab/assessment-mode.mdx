An assessment is a timed, graded set of one or more lab scenarios that a recruiter
has invited you to. It uses the same lab workspace as any other lab, with a few
differences: a countdown timer, no visible points, no check button, and grading
that happens when the assessment ends.

**Before you start:**

- Accept your invitation. See [Accept an invitation](/en/learners/accept-invitation/).
- Use a recent desktop browser on a stable connection, with enough time to finish
  in one sitting. The countdown does not pause.
- Read [The lab workspace](/en/lab/overview/) and
  [Use the lab terminal](/en/lab/terminal/) if you have not used a Klastro lab
  before.

## 1. Wait in the lobby

Opening the assessment takes you to its lobby. It lists the assessment's
scenarios and prepares an environment for each one.

<Steps>

1. If the lobby shows **Waiting for Authorization**, the recruiter has not
   approved the assessment yet. Keep the page open: it updates by itself once
   they do.

2. Watch each scenario card. While its environment is being prepared, it shows
   three stages — **Reserve environment**, **Start machines**,
   **Install scenario** — then **Ready**. The heading counts them, for example
   **2 of 3 ready**. Larger environments can take several minutes.

3. Each card also shows the scenario's difficulty and estimated duration, for
   example **Est. 30m**.

4. When every scenario is **Ready**, the **Enter** buttons become active. Click
   **Enter** on the scenario you want to start with.

</Steps>

:::note
The countdown does **not** start while you wait in the lobby. It starts when you
enter your first scenario, as the lobby says under
**Start Whenever You’re Ready**. You can do the scenarios in any order.
:::

If a scenario shows **Could not be prepared**, you cannot fix it from your side.
Contact the recruiter who invited you. See also
[Lab provisioning troubleshooting](/en/troubleshooting/provisioning/).

## 2. Work in the lab

Once you enter, the top bar shows the assessment timer, and the lab works as
described in [The lab workspace](/en/lab/overview/), with these differences:

| | In an assessment |
|---|---|
| Points | Not shown. You are scored, but the numbers are for the recruiter. |
| Checking your work | There is no check button. Every task is checked automatically when the assessment ends. |
| Hints | Available, in a chain. Each one you reveal reduces your score for that task, even though the cost is not shown. |
| Notes | Available, and shared with the recruiter in your report. See [Take notes during a lab](/en/lab/notes/). |
| Moving between scenarios | Use the scenario selector in the Instructions header, or **Next** on a scenario's last task (**Next Scenario?** → **Yes, continue**). You can go back the same way. |

See [Tasks, checks and hints](/en/lab/tasks-and-checks/) for how grading works.

## The timer

Before you enter the first scenario, the top bar shows the total duration, for
example **Duration: 1h30m**, with **Timer starts on first scenario** under it.
Once you enter, it counts down in hours, minutes and seconds.

| Timer colour | Time left |
|---|---|
| Neutral | More than 15 minutes |
| Amber | 15 minutes or less |
| Red | 5 minutes or less |

There is one timer for the whole assessment, shared by all its scenarios. It is
kept by Klastro, not by your browser: reloading the page, switching scenarios or
leaving the lab does not stop or reset it.

:::caution
**Exit lab** in the top bar takes you out of the lab but does not pause the
assessment. The countdown keeps running while you are away.
:::

## Rules of conduct

Klastro does not set rules of its own beyond the lab itself; the recruiter who
invited you may give you instructions with the invitation. Whatever those are,
keep in mind:

- **Your environment is the answer.** What counts is the state of your lab when
  the assessment ends, not the commands you typed. Leave it in its final, working
  state.
- **Hints are recorded.** Every hint you reveal is kept and reduces your score
  for that task.
- **Your notes are read.** The recruiter sees your notepad in your report.
- **Your terminal can be watched.** People from the recruiting organization can
  follow your terminal tabs live, in read-only mode. They cannot type in your
  terminal, and nothing on your screen tells you when they are watching.

### Webcam, microphone and screen

The top bar shows a camera icon labelled **Webcam off**. In the current version,
the lab does not ask for access to your webcam, microphone or screen, and does
not record them. If your browser asks for one of these permissions on a Klastro
page, see [Webcam troubleshooting](/en/troubleshooting/webcam/). Recruiters can
find what they can follow in
[Proctoring and supervision](/en/recruiters/proctoring-supervision/).

## 3. Finish the assessment

When you have done all you can, in every scenario:

<Steps>

1. Click **Complete Assessment** in the top bar.

2. Read the confirmation. It warns that **You will not be able to return to this
   lab once you finish.**

3. Click **Yes, Complete Assessment**.

</Steps>

The screen shows **Assessment Completed** and
**Your work has been saved and the recruiter will receive the final results shortly.**
A few seconds later you are taken back to the dashboard. Klastro then checks
every task of every scenario against your environments and sends the results to
the recruiter.

:::danger
**Complete Assessment** ends the whole assessment, not just the scenario you are
on. Check that you have worked through every scenario first.
:::

## If time runs out

When the countdown reaches zero, the timer turns red and shows **Expired**, and
the assessment ends on its own:

- The screen shows **Time is up!** and
  **Your assessment has timed out. Your work has been saved and the recruiter will receive the final results shortly.**
- You are taken back to the dashboard a few seconds later.
- Every task is checked against your environments as they were when time ran
  out. Work you had already done counts, exactly as if you had clicked
  **Complete Assessment**.

:::tip
Partial credit is real: each check a task passes earns its points. In the last
minutes, make sure what you have done is in place and saved rather than starting
something new.
:::

If the screen shows **Assessment Failed** instead, the assessment hit a technical
problem on Klastro's side. Your work has been saved; contact the recruiter who
invited you.
