Every Klastro lab opens in the same full-screen workspace, whether you are
following a tutorial, practising a challenge, exploring a playground or sitting
an assessment. This page explains how that screen is laid out and what each part
does. The pages that follow go into each part in detail.

## The layout at a glance

The workspace is a single card split into two panes, with a thin bar above it and
another below it.

| Area | Where | What it holds |
|---|---|---|
| Top bar | Above the workspace | The Klastro logo, the scenario name, the assessment timer, **Report a problem**, language and theme toggles, and the way out of the lab |
| Left pane | Left side of the card | The **Instructions** tab (tasks, hints, checks) and, where available, the **Notes** tab |
| Right pane | Right side of the card | Terminal tabs, plus the **Code** tab and app view tabs when the scenario provides them |
| Status bar | Below the workspace | **Connected** or **Waiting to start session...**, and the scenario name |

A playground has no instructions: the left pane is removed and the terminal takes
the whole card.

## The left pane

### Instructions

The **Instructions** tab is where the scenario tells you what to do. From top to
bottom it shows:

- **A header** with the list toggle (**Show tasks** / **Hide tasks**, or
  **Show steps** / **Hide steps** in a tutorial), your progress as
  *done of total* (for example **2 of 8**), and, on a challenge, the scenario's
  total points.
- **About this scenario**, a collapsed section with the scenario description.
- **Connect to the machines**, a collapsed section that only appears when your
  lab has machines you reach over ssh. See [Use the lab terminal](/en/lab/terminal/).
- **The task list** down the side, numbered. A tick replaces the number once a
  step has passed its check.
- **The current task**: its position (for example **3 of 8**), title, instructions,
  the check control in a tutorial, and hints.
- **Previous** and **Next** buttons at the bottom. On the last task they become
  **Finish & submit** (challenge) or **Finish** (tutorial).

Commands in the instructions are copy buttons: click any inline command or the
**Copy** button on a code block, then paste it in the terminal.

While your environment is still being built, the Instructions tab shows
**Preparing your environment** with a progress bar and the setup steps instead of
the tasks. Tasks only appear once there is a working environment to run them
against.

How tasks, hints and checks work is covered in
[Tasks, checks and hints](/en/lab/tasks-and-checks/).

### Notes

The **Notes** tab is a plain-text scratchpad that saves itself as you type. It
is available in tutorials, challenges and assessments. In an assessment, the
recruiter can read it in your report. See [Take notes during a lab](/en/lab/notes/).

## The right pane

The right pane is a strip of tabs over one large surface.

- **Shell** tabs (**Shell 1**, **Shell 2**…) are terminals in your lab. Use the
  **+** button to open more, up to five.
- **Node** tabs (**Node**, or **Node 1**, **Node 2**…) appear when your lab has
  machines. Each one opens a terminal directly on that machine.
- **Code** opens an in-browser VS Code editor, when the scenario includes one.
- **App view** tabs, named after the application (for example a GitOps or
  monitoring console), embed web tools running inside your lab.

A small dot on each terminal tab shows its connection: green when connected, red
on an error, grey when closed, and pulsing while connecting. A pulsing dot on an
app view tab means the application is still starting.

See [Use the lab terminal](/en/lab/terminal/) and
[Use the editor and app views](/en/lab/ide-and-app-views/).

## Resize the two panes

Drag the grip between the left and right panes to give more room to the
instructions or to the terminal. The left pane can take between roughly a fifth
and a little over half of the width.

To resize from the keyboard, press <kbd>Tab</kbd> until the grip (labelled
**Resize panels**) is focused, then use <kbd>←</kbd> and <kbd>→</kbd>.

:::note
The split position is not remembered: reloading the page resets it. Whether the
task list is collapsed, and which sections are open, is remembered for the rest
of your visit in that browser tab.
:::

## The top bar

The top bar changes with the kind of lab.

**In every lab**

- The **Klastro logo** takes you back to the dashboard.
- The **scenario name** appears next to it on wide screens.
- **Report a problem** opens a support form with this lab attached. See
  [Report a problem with a lab](/en/support/report-lab-problem/).
- The language and theme toggles. The terminal always stays dark, whatever the
  theme.

**In a tutorial, challenge or playground**

- **Back to dashboard** (the dashboard icon) leaves the lab and keeps it running.
- **Leave lab** (the exit icon) asks whether to **Pause & resume later** or
  **End session**. See [What persists](#what-persists) below.
- If you opened the lab from a certification path, a button takes you back to
  that path.
- If the lab belongs to an instructor-led class, an ask-for-help button lets you
  raise your hand without leaving the lab.

**In an assessment**

- The **countdown timer** sits in the middle.
- **Complete Assessment** ends the whole assessment.
- **Exit lab** leaves the lab. The countdown keeps running while you are away.

See [Take an assessment](/en/lab/assessment-mode/).

:::note
The lab bar does not show your credit balance or a live score. On a challenge,
the points each task is worth appear in the Instructions pane, and your graded
score appears on the results page after you submit. Credits are on the
dashboard; see [Plans and credits](/en/billing/plans-and-credits/).
:::

## What persists

| You do this | What happens to your work |
|---|---|
| Reload the page, or lose your connection briefly | Your shells keep running in the lab. Each terminal tab reconnects to the same shell, with the same commands still running. Your open tabs are restored in the same browser tab. |
| Click **Back to dashboard** | Nothing stops. The lab keeps running (and, on a subscription, keeps using credits). Open it again from **My Sessions**. |
| **Leave lab** → **Pause & resume later** | The environment is kept and stops running. Opening the lab again resumes it. |
| **Leave lab** → **End session** | The environment is destroyed for good. This cannot be undone. |
| Notes | Saved automatically to your account, about a second after you stop typing. |
| Hints you have revealed | Recorded. They stay revealed after a reload, on any device. |
| Checks you have run | Recorded. Passed steps stay ticked after a reload. |

:::caution
**End session** destroys the environment and everything in it. If you might come
back, choose **Pause & resume later** instead.
:::

A lab can also end without you: when an instructor ends a class, the screen shows
**This environment has ended**, and checks you already passed are kept. When an
instructor moves you to another step of a class, the screen shows
**This environment is paused** and your work is kept until they bring you back.
