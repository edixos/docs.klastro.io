The **Learn** menu in the sidebar gathers everything you can launch on your own: **Tutorials**, **Challenges**, **Playgrounds** and **Certification Paths**. If you are new, start with [Your first lab](/en/learners/first-lab/).

## Find a scenario in the catalog

<Steps>

1. Under **Learn**, click **Tutorials**, **Challenges** or **Playgrounds**. The catalog opens, filtered on that product type.

2. Narrow the list down:
   - the **All products**, **All levels** and **All sources** menus filter by type, difficulty and origin (**Official** or **Studio**);
   - the **Search scenarios…** field searches by name;
   - click a tag on a card to filter on it, and **Clear tags** to get the full list back;
   - the sort menu offers **Fastest start**, **Name (A–Z)**, **Difficulty** and **Shortest first**. **Fastest start** is the default: scenarios with a pre-warmed environment come first (see [Fast start](#fast-start)), then the newest. The catalog remembers the order you pick;
   - **Group by tag** arranges the cards by topic.

3. Click **Explore** on a card to open the scenario page: estimated time, environment, task list, scoring.

</Steps>

Each card opens with the logo of the technology the scenario is about: Kubernetes, Linux, Argo, Prometheus and so on. A scenario that names no known technology shows the Klastro logo.

Each card carries a **Free** or **Pro** badge. If your plan does not include a **Pro** scenario, its page shows **Upgrade to launch** instead of the launch button. See [Plans and credits](/en/billing/plans-and-credits/).

## Fast start

Some scenarios have a pre-warmed environment, ready before you click the launch button. Their card and page show a flame icon. The fuller the flame, the sooner your lab is ready. Hover it to read what it promises:

| Flame | Label | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Filled | **Starts instantly** | An environment is already running. The lab starts almost immediately. |
| Half filled | **Starts fast: a paused environment resumes** | A paused environment wakes up for you. Faster than a regular launch, not instant. |
| Outline | **Starts faster: environment pre-built** | The environment is built and still has to boot. Faster than a regular launch. |

To see only these scenarios, click the **Fast start** chip in the catalog filters. It only appears when at least one scenario in the list has a pre-warmed environment.

When a lab starts from a pre-warmed environment, the flame also shows in the lab's top bar. The lab is ready much sooner: typically 30 to 60 seconds for Linux machines, instead of several minutes. Preparation steps that were already done are not shown again.

:::note[Availability is not guaranteed]
The flame reflects availability when the catalog loaded. If the pre-warmed environment has since run out, your lab starts normally, cold: it takes longer, but nothing else changes.
:::

## Take a challenge

A challenge is a real task, graded on your environment. There is no step-by-step checking: your work is graded when you submit it.

<Steps>

1. Open a challenge’s page and click **Start Practice**.

2. Wait for setup to finish, then read the **Tasks** in the instructions panel. Each task shows how many points it is worth.

3. Complete the tasks in the terminal. Move between them with **Next** and **Previous**.

4. On the last task, click **Finish & submit**.

5. In the **Finish and submit for grading?** window, click **Submit & see results**. To keep going a little longer, click **Keep working**.

</Steps>

Klastro validates every task against your running environment, then shows your results. To read them, see [Results and history](/en/learners/results-history/).

:::caution[Hints cost points]
In a challenge, a hint is uncovered with **Reveal**, and its cost shows beside it, for example **(−5 pts)**. Hints unlock in order: **Reveal hint 1 first** before the next one.
:::

:::note
After you submit, your session keeps running until you end it. Remember to release it: see [Manage your sessions](/en/learners/manage-sessions/).
:::

## Explore a playground

A playground is a blank environment with no tasks and no grading. Open its page, click **Launch Playground**, then use it however you like. There is nothing to submit: when you are done, end the session.

## One lab per scenario

You can only have one running lab per scenario. If you already have one, the launch button turns into **Resume** (or **View progress** while it is still being prepared) and takes you back into that lab.

Your plan also limits how many sessions run at once. When you hit that limit, the **You’re at your session limit** window lists what is **Running now**: click **Pause** or **End** on one of them, then **Try again**.

## Follow a certification path

A certification path is a roadmap of steps from the first concept to exam readiness.

<Steps>

1. Under **Learn**, click **Certification Paths**.

2. Filter by **Topic** and **Level** if you like, then click **Start** on a path (or **Resume** if you have begun it).

3. The path page shows your **Progress** and the list of steps. Each step is **Ready**, **Locked**, **Completed** or **Unavailable**.

4. On a **Ready** step, click the launch button to open the matching lab.

5. Once the step is done, click **Mark complete**. You can take it back with **Undo**.

</Steps>

A few rules worth knowing:

- A **Locked** step shows **Complete first:** followed by its prerequisite steps.
- An **Unavailable** step points to a scenario that is not in your organization’s catalog.
- In a lab launched from a path, the **Back to path** button takes you back to it; the session keeps running.
- When you finish a tutorial launched from a path, the step is ticked automatically and the environment is ended.

:::note[Milestones, not credentials]
Progress is self-declared: these are milestones, not credentials. The paths are independent practice, not affiliated with or endorsed by the certification bodies.
:::
