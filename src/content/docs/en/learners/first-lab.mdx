In this tutorial you launch a guided tutorial on a real cluster, check each step, then release the environment. By the end you will know how to launch, use and stop any Klastro lab.

**What you need**: a Klastro account and an organization (see [Create an account and sign in](/en/getting-started/create-account/)), and credits available in your balance (see [Plans and credits](/en/billing/plans-and-credits/)).

## 1. Pick a tutorial

<Steps>

1. In the sidebar, open **Learn**, then click **Tutorials**.

   The catalog opens, filtered on tutorials. Each card shows the level (**Beginner**, **Intermediate**, **Advanced**) and the number of tasks.

2. Pick a **Beginner** tutorial and click **Explore**.

3. On the scenario page, look over the **Estimated time**, the **Environment** and the list of **Guided steps**. You now know what lies ahead.

</Steps>

:::note
An **Upgrade to launch** button in place of the launch button means the scenario is reserved for the Pro plan. Pick another one marked **Free**.
:::

## 2. Launch the environment

<Steps>

1. Click **Start Tutorial**.

2. Klastro opens the lab page and shows **Preparing your environment**. The stages go by: **Reserving your environment…**, **Starting the machines…**, then **Setting up your lab…**.

   Depending on the environment, this takes from a few seconds to a few minutes. Stay on the page: it turns into your workspace on its own.

3. When the environment is ready, the workspace appears: **Instructions** on one side, the terminal on the other.

</Steps>

## 3. Complete the first step

<Steps>

1. Read the first step in the **Instructions** panel.

2. Click into the terminal and type the commands you are asked for. To start, try:

   ```sh
   kubectl get nodes
   ```

   You are connected to a real cluster: the command lists its nodes.

3. Once you have done the step, click **Check this step**. Klastro inspects your environment and shows **Step complete**.

   If you see **Not yet — try again**, reread the step, fix things, then click **Check again**.

</Steps>

:::tip[Stuck?]
Open the **Hints** under the step and click **Show**. Hints are free in a tutorial: use as many as you need.
:::

## 4. See it through

<Steps>

1. Click **Next** to move to the next step, and repeat: read, do, check.

2. On the last step, click **Finish**. The message **Tutorial complete. Nice work.** appears, and Klastro takes you back to the tutorials catalog.

</Steps>

## 5. Release the environment

Finishing a tutorial does not destroy its environment: it keeps running, and drawing credits, until you end it.

<Steps>

1. In the **Personal** section of the sidebar, click **My Sessions**. Your tutorial is at the top of the list.

2. Click the **Session actions** menu (the three dots) at the end of the row, then **Delete**.

3. Confirm with **Delete session**. The row shows **Removing…**, then moves to the **Ended** sessions.

</Steps>

Congratulations: you launched a real environment, validated your work and released the resources.
