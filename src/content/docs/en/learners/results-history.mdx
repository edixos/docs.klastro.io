Challenges are the only self-serve labs that produce a score. Klastro keeps every graded run, with its per-task breakdown, long after the environment is gone.

:::note
Tutorials and playgrounds are not graded. Assessment results go to the recruiter, not to this page.
:::

## Read a challenge’s results

The **Results** page opens automatically when you click **Submit & see results** at the end of a challenge (see [Take a challenge](/en/learners/catalog-challenges-paths/#take-a-challenge)).

While grading runs, the page shows **Validating your work against the live environment…** and each task carries a **Grading…** badge. Stay on the page: it updates on its own.

Once grading is done, you see:

- your **Score**, in points;
- the number of **Tasks passed**;
- for each task (**Task 1**, **Task 2**…), the outcome **Passed** or **Not passed**.

From this page:

- **More challenges** opens the challenges catalog;
- **My challenges** opens your history;
- **End session** destroys the challenge’s environment, which keeps running after you submit.

:::tip
A task **Not passed**? Your session is still running: you can go back into the lab, work out what was missing, and start a fresh run later. End the session when you are done so it stops drawing credits.
:::

## Find the results of an ended session

<Steps>

1. In the **Personal** section of the sidebar, click **My Sessions**.

2. Click the **Ended** filter.

3. On a challenge’s row, click **Results**.

</Steps>

## Browse your challenge history

<Steps>

1. In the **Personal** section of the sidebar, click **My Results**. The **Challenges** page lists all your graded runs.

2. Each row shows the challenge, the **Score** and the number of **Tasks** passed.

3. Click a row to expand its task-by-task breakdown.

</Steps>

This history is kept even after each session ends: it is the lasting record of what you passed. To start a new challenge, click **New challenge**.

If the page says **No challenges completed yet**, finish a challenge and submit it for grading: it will show up here.
