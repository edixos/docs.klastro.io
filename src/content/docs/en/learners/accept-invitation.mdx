A recruiter invited you to a technical assessment on Klastro. This guide takes you from the invitation email to the assessment lobby, where you start your scenarios.

**What you need**: the invitation email, a recent browser on a computer and a stable internet connection. You need no plan and no credits.

## 1. Open the link in the email

<Tabs>
<TabItem label="You don’t have an account yet">

The email contains a link to activate your account.

<Steps>

1. Open the link. The **Access your account** page appears.

2. Enter the email address the invitation was sent to and click **Send code**.

3. Enter the **Verification code** from the email, then click **Verify & continue**.

4. On **Welcome to the platform 🎉**, check your details, choose a password and click **Activate account**.

</Steps>

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="You already have an account">

The email contains a link to your invitations in the inviting organization. Open it and sign in if needed.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

The first time you sign in, accept the terms of use with **Accept and continue** (see [Create an account and sign in](/en/getting-started/create-account/#accept-the-terms-of-use)).

If several organizations have invited you, the **Your Organizations** page appears: pick the one whose invitations you want to see.

## 2. Accept the invitation

The **Your Invitations** page lists all your invitations, headed by the number that are **Pending**, **Accepted** and **Expired**. Each invitation shows when you received it and when it expires.

<Steps>

1. Find the invitation for the assessment you were told about.

2. Click **Accept**. The message **Invitation accepted!** confirms it.

3. Click the eye-shaped button on that invitation to open its details.

</Steps>

:::caution[Expiry date]
An invitation has a deadline, set by the recruiter — seven days by default. After it passes, the invitation shows as expired and can no longer be started: ask the organization for a new one. When less than a week is left, the invitation page shows **Expiring Soon**.
:::

## 3. Read the details before you start

The invitation page shows:

- the assessment **Duration**, the number of **Scenarios** and whether the **Webcam** is **Required** or **Optional**;
- the list of **Assessment Scenarios**, with their estimated time;
- the **Important Information** box.

:::danger[The timer does not stop]
Once started, the timer cannot be paused. If the assessment has several scenarios, one timer covers all of them. Set aside the stated duration of uninterrupted time before you start.
:::

Klastro does not ask for access to your camera; follow the recruiter's instructions. See [Webcam and permissions](/en/troubleshooting/webcam/).

## 4. Start and enter the lobby

<Steps>

1. Click **Start Assessment**. Klastro takes you to the assessment lobby.

2. If the assessment must be authorized by a recruiter, the lobby shows **Waiting for Authorization**. Stay on the page: your scenarios are prepared as soon as it is authorized.

3. The lobby prepares each scenario: **Reserve environment**, **Start machines**, **Install scenario**. A counter shows how many are ready.

4. When every scenario shows **Ready**, click **Enter** on the scenario you want to start with.

</Steps>

:::tip[The timer starts when you enter]
The global assessment countdown does **not** start until you enter your first scenario. Take your time to settle in the lobby. You can complete the scenarios in any order you prefer.
:::

You are now in the lab. What is specific to assessments — the timer, moving from one scenario to the next, completing the assessment — is covered in [Assessment mode](/en/lab/assessment-mode/).

## Resume an assessment you started

If you left the page part-way through, go back to the invitation page: it shows **Assessment in progress**, and **Resume Assessment** takes you back to the lobby. The timer kept running in the meantime.

Once the assessment is over, the page button reads **Assessment Completed**; your results are processed and sent to the recruiter, and your score may show on this page.

## If something goes wrong

- **Invitation Revoked** or **Invitation Expired**: contact the organization that invited you.
- The lobby shows **Could not be prepared** for a scenario: contact the recruiter who invited you.
- Anything else: [Invitation problems](/en/troubleshooting/invitations/) and [Lab won’t start](/en/troubleshooting/provisioning/).
