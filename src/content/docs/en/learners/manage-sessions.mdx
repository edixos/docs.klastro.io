Every lab you launch creates a session: a real environment that runs, and draws credits, as long as it is neither paused nor ended. This guide shows you how to stay in control of your sessions.

## Open My sessions

In the **Personal** section of the sidebar, click **My Sessions**. The page lists every lab you have started, most recent first, each with:

- the **Scenario**, its type, difficulty and number of tasks;
- its **Status**: **Starting**, **Provisioning**, **Ready**, **Paused** or **Ended**;
- the **Score** for challenges;
- when it **Started**, as a relative time for a running session (“23 minutes ago”) and as a date once it has ended.

The **All**, **Active** and **Ended** filters narrow the list.

## Get back into a lab

On a running session, click the row or the button in the **Action** column:

- **Open** for a ready session;
- **Resume** for a paused session, which starts up again first;
- **View progress** for a session still being prepared.

You can also get back in from the scenario’s page in the catalog: the launch button turns into **Resume** when you already have a session for that scenario.

## Pause a session

Pausing stops the environment while keeping your work. Paused time does not draw credits, and a paused session does not count towards your limit of concurrent sessions.

<Steps>

1. In the lab, click **Leave lab** in the top bar.

2. In the **Leave this lab?** window, click **Pause & resume later**.

</Steps>

From **My Sessions**, open the **Session actions** menu (the three dots) of a ready session and click **Pause**.

To pick it back up, click **Resume** in **My Sessions** or on the scenario’s page. You find your environment exactly as you left it.

:::note
If resuming is refused because too many sessions are running, or because you have reached your plan’s limit, pause or end another session and try again.
:::

## Leave the lab without stopping anything

The lab’s **Back to dashboard** icon takes you to the dashboard and leaves the session running. It is handy for a short break, but the environment keeps drawing credits.

## End a session

Ending a session destroys its environment for good. Checks you already passed and challenge results you already submitted are kept.

<Steps>

1. In the lab, click **Leave lab**, then **End session**.

   Or, from **My Sessions**, open the **Session actions** menu, click **Delete**, then confirm with **Delete session**.

2. The session shows **Removing…** while the environment is destroyed, then moves to the **Ended** sessions.

</Steps>

:::danger[This can’t be undone]
An ended session cannot be resumed. Anything not saved elsewhere — files, cluster resources — is lost. When in doubt, choose **Pause & resume later**.
:::

## How long a session lives

Each scenario sets a maximum lifetime for its sessions. Paused time is not counted. When that lifetime runs out, the session ends on its own and the lab shows **This environment has ended**: the environment no longer accepts commands, but any checks you had already passed were recorded.

A session can also be paused automatically when your credits run out. See [Credits and automatic pauses](/en/troubleshooting/credits-and-pauses/).
