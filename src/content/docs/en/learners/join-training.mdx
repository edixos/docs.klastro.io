In an instructor-led class, the instructor moves the whole group from one scenario to the next. This guide is for participants: how to find the class, open your environment at the right moment and stay in touch with your instructor.

**What you need**: a member account in the organization running the class. The instructor enrols you; you do not pay for your place.

## Get into your account

If you did not have a Klastro account yet, being enrolled creates one and you receive a **Join … on Klastro** email. Click **Set up my account**, choose a password, then click **Activate account** (see [Create an account and sign in](/en/getting-started/create-account/)).

If you already had an account, just sign in: the class shows up in your organization.

## Find your class

<Steps>

1. In the sidebar, open **Train**, then click **My Training**.

2. Your classes are sorted into three sections: **Happening now**, **Upcoming** and **Finished**. Each card shows the instructor and the time.

3. Click the class to open it.

</Steps>

:::note
If the page says **You are not on a class yet**, the instructor has not added you yet, or you are in a different organization. Check the organization selected in the sidebar.
:::

## Start your environment

Before the class begins, it shows **Not started** and the message **Your environment opens when your instructor starts the session.**

<Steps>

1. When the instructor starts the class, the status switches to **Live**. No need to reload the page.

2. Click **Start my environment**. Your environment is built at that moment, just for you.

3. Click **Open my environment**. The lab opens on the current scenario.

</Steps>

To work inside the lab, see [The lab environment](/en/lab/overview/) and [Tasks and checks](/en/lab/tasks-and-checks/).

## Follow the programme

The **Programme** section lists the class’s scenarios, in order. During the class, the instructor moves everyone from one scenario to the next; **you are here** marks the current step.

When the instructor moves you to another step, the environment of the previous step is set aside. If you go back to it, the lab shows **This environment is paused**: your work is kept, and the environment starts again when the instructor brings you back to that step. Click **Back to the class** to return to the class page.

The lab’s **Leave lab** button takes you back to the class page, not to the dashboard.

## Ask for help

<Steps>

1. Click **Ask for help**, on the class page or in the lab.

2. Optionally describe what is blocking you in **What has you stuck**. The field is optional: a hand up with nothing written on it is still a hand up.

3. Confirm with **Ask for help**. The button reads **Help requested** while your request is open.

</Steps>

Your instructor sees the request on their class monitor. They can connect into your environment to help: the lab then shows **Your instructor has joined this environment to help you.** If you no longer need help, click **Never mind**.

:::tip
**Ask for help** sits right next to **Open my environment**: even if your environment does not come up, you can still reach your instructor.
:::

## After the class

When the instructor closes the session, the page shows **This class has ended**: the environments are stopped and no scenario can be picked back up.

- The class **Resources** stay available.
- The **Your feedback** section lets you rate the class from 1 to 5 and add a **Comment**. Tick the attribution box if you want the instructor to know the feedback is yours; otherwise they see the rating and comment without your name. Send it with **Send my feedback**; you can change it later with **Update my feedback**.
