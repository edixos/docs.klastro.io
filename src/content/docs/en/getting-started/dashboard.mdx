The **Dashboard** is the page you land on after signing in. It is the same page for everyone, but what it shows depends on your organization, your role and your seat: you only see sections for things you can actually use.

Under the title, a line names your organization and its plan (**Enterprise** for an Enterprise contract). The button at the top right is your most likely next step: **Invite candidate** if you run assessments, **Browse catalog** otherwise.

## Needs you

The first section lists what is waiting for you, most urgent first: something that blocks you, then something happening right now, then something to do, then a heads-up. Each row is one sentence and one button that takes you where you can act. The first four rows show; **Show N more** reveals the rest.

When nothing is waiting, the section says **Nothing needs you right now.**

| Row | Button | Who sees it |
|---|---|---|
| **The last payment for your plan failed.** | **Update payment** | Admins and Billing Admins |
| **You are out of credits. New labs cannot start.** | **Add credits** | Everyone, except in an Enterprise contract |
| **A cohort is live.** | **Open** | Instructors |
| **A candidate is taking an assessment.** | **Supervise** | Recruiters |
| **A result is waiting for your verdict.** | **Review** | Recruiters |
| **A paid seat is not assigned to anyone.** | **Assign** | Admins and Billing Admins |
| **You have less than a quarter of your credits left.** | **Add credits** | Everyone, except in an Enterprise contract |
| **A lab failed in the last 24 hours.** | **View** | Everyone |
| **An invitation expires within 48 hours.** | **View** | Recruiters |
| **Your temporary access ends on** a date | **See plans** | Members with temporary access, in its last 7 days |
| **Your plan ends on** a date | **Manage** | Admins and Billing Admins, once a cancellation is scheduled |
| **Your plan renews on** a date | **Manage** | Admins and Billing Admins, in the last 7 days before renewal |

When there are several of the same thing, the row gives the count: **3 results are waiting for your verdict.**

## Pick up where you left off

Your labs that are still running, with a button to go back into each one. A lab that is still being prepared opens on its setup progress. See [Manage your sessions](/en/learners/manage-sessions/).

If you are enrolled in an instructor-led class, **Your classes** follows. See [Join a training session](/en/learners/join-training/).

## Your work

The main column shows the work your role is for:

- **Latest results** (recruiters): the latest finished assessments with **Candidate**, **Score**, **Verdict** and **Finished**. A result without a verdict reads **To review**. Click a candidate to open their report. See [Record a verdict and filter reports](/en/recruiters/verdicts/).
- **Cohorts** (instructors): classes running now, then the next ones, with **Status**, **Trainees** and **Starts**. With none planned, **New cohort** starts one. See [Create a cohort](/en/instructors/cohorts/).
- **Paths in progress**: the certification paths you have started and not finished, with your progress. See [Catalog, challenges and paths](/en/learners/catalog-challenges-paths/).
- **Your labs**: the labs you launched recently. Before your first lab, **Start practising** shows the three ways to practise instead.

## Your account

The side column sums up what you hold:

- **Plan** and **Seat**. Admins and Billing Admins also see when the plan renews or ends.
- **Credits**: your own balance, a bar that turns amber below a quarter and red when empty, the date of the **Next top-up**, and **Add credits**. Not shown in an Enterprise contract.
- **Labs running** against your plan's limit, and **Labs completed**.
- **Seats** (Admins and Billing Admins): for each seat type you bought, how many are assigned, with **Manage seats**.
- **Shortcuts** to the pages your role and seat open.

See [Plans and credits](/en/billing/plans-and-credits/) and [Manage seats](/en/billing/seats/).

## Connect your AI assistant

This card gives you the command that adds Klastro to your AI assistant. Pick **Claude**, **Codex**, **Copilot** or **Gemini**, click the command to copy it, and paste it in a terminal. The line under it says how to sign in. For every client and what the assistant can then do, see [Connect your AI client](/en/mcp/connect-clients/).
