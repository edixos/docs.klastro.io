Klastro serves very different people, and the app only shows each of them what concerns them. Get your bearings from what you see in the sidebar, then follow the pages suggested for you.

## What the sidebar shows

The sidebar groups the app by product. A group only appears when your organization and your seat give you access to it.

| Group | Contents | Who sees it |
|---|---|---|
| **Learn** | **Tutorials**, **Challenges**, **Playgrounds**, **Certification Paths** | Everyone |
| **Train** | **My Training**, **Cohorts**, **Tracks** | **My Training** for everyone; **Cohorts** and **Tracks** with an Instructor seat |
| **Assess** | **Templates**, **Assessments**, **Results**, **Invitations** | Organizations on an Enterprise contract |
| **Studio** | **Scenario Studio** | Members allowed to author scenarios |
| **Personal** | **Profile**, **My Sessions**, **My Results** | Everyone |
| **Organization** | **Billing**, **Team**, **Audit Log**, **Settings**, **Help Desk** | **Billing** and **Help Desk** for everyone; the rest for administrators |
