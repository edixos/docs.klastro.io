This guide covers everything between landing on [app.klastro.io](https://app.klastro.io) and reaching your first dashboard.

:::note[Private preview]
Klastro is currently in private preview: **registration is by invitation**. Without a valid invitation, the registration page shows **Registration is by invitation** instead of the form. You can create your account from the link in your invitation email.
:::

## Create your account

How you create your account depends on the invitation you received.

<Tabs>
<TabItem label="Invitation to Klastro">

You received a personal invitation to join the platform.

<Steps>

1. Open the link in your invitation email. The **Your invitation to Klastro** page shows the invited address.

2. Click **Continue to registration**.

3. On the **Create your account** page, enter your **Full name** and a **Password** of at least 8 characters. Register with the email address the invitation was sent to.

4. Wait while **Running a quick security check…** completes, then click **Create account**.

</Steps>

You then move on to [creating your organization](#create-or-choose-an-organization).

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Invitation from an organization">

Your organization created an account for you and invited you to join.

<Steps>

1. Open the link in the email. The **Join … on Klastro** page shows the organization’s name.

2. Click **Set up my account**.

3. On the **Welcome to the platform 🎉** screen, check your details under **Your information**, then choose a strong password under **Secure your account**.

4. Click **Activate account**.

</Steps>

You land straight in the organization that invited you.

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Invitation to an assessment">

A recruiter invited you to a technical assessment. Follow the dedicated guide: [Accept an assessment invitation](/en/learners/accept-invitation/).

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

:::caution[An invitation link that no longer works]
Invitation links stop working after a few days, and each link works once. If the page shows **This invitation has expired**, **This invitation was already used**, **This invitation was withdrawn** or **A newer invitation was sent**, use the link in your most recent email or ask for a new invitation. See [Invitation problems](/en/troubleshooting/invitations/).
:::

## Accept the terms of use

The first time you sign in, Klastro shows the **Terms of Use and Privacy Policy** screen. It sums up in four points what you are agreeing to — credit usage, ownership of your content, hosting in France, GDPR rights — and links to the full documents, which are what govern.

<Steps>

1. Read the summary and, if you wish, open the full documents (they open in a new tab).

2. Click **Accept and continue**. You are taken back to where you were going.

</Steps>

If you would rather not accept, click **Log out**: the app cannot be used until you accept.

:::note
This screen comes back whenever a new version of the terms or the privacy policy is published, even in the middle of your work. Your acceptance is recorded once per version.
:::

## Create or choose an organization

Everything in Klastro happens inside an organization. After registering:

- if you belong to **no** organization, you land on **Create your Organization**;
- if you belong to **several** organizations, you land on **Select Organization**;
- if you belong to exactly one, you go straight to your dashboard.

### Create your organization

<Steps>

1. Enter the **Organization name**. The **Workspace URL** fills itself in; change it if you like.

2. Optionally choose your **Country**.

3. If this is a company, tick **This organization is a company** and fill in, if you have them, the **SIRET**, **Billing address** and **VAT number**. Leave the box unticked for a school, a community or a personal workspace.

4. Click **Create organisation**.

</Steps>

If an organization with the same name already exists, Klastro shows **… is already on Klastro**. Click **Notify my admin** to ask them for an invitation, or **Create a new organisation instead**.

:::tip
Looking to join your team rather than create an organization? Ask your administrator to invite you: you will be added automatically.
:::

### Choose an organization

On **Select Organization**, click the organization you want to open. Use **Search organizations...** when the list is long, or **New Organization** to create another one.

Once inside the app, click the current organization’s name in the sidebar: the **Switch Organization** menu lists the other organizations you belong to.

## Sign in

<Steps>

1. Open [app.klastro.io](https://app.klastro.io) and click **Sign In**.

2. On **Welcome back**, enter your email address and password, or use one of the **Continue with …** buttons if they are offered.

3. Click **Sign In**.

</Steps>

## Recover access to your account

If you forgot your password, Klastro emails you a one-time access code.

<Steps>

1. Open [app.klastro.io/auth/recovery](https://app.klastro.io/auth/recovery). The **Access your account** page appears.

2. Enter your **Email address** and click **Send code**.

3. On **Check your email**, enter the **Verification code** from the email, then click **Verify & continue**.

4. Choose a new password under **Secure your account**, then click **Activate account**.

</Steps>

:::tip
To change your password while signed in, use the same procedure: the **Profile** page only edits your name, email and photo.
:::

Seeing another error while signing in (**Session expired**, **Security check failed**, **Flow expired**)? See [Sign-in problems](/en/troubleshooting/sign-in/).
