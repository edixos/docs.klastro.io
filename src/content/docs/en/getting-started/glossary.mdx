Terms in bold are the ones the app displays.

## Labs and scenarios

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Scenario** | The definition of a lab: its environment, what is installed at the start, its tasks and its scoring. A scenario is **Official** (written by the Klastro team) or **Studio** (authored by your organization). |
| **Lab** | General word for any environment you launch, whatever its type. |
| **Session** | Your running copy of a scenario: a real, isolated environment built for you and destroyed when it ends. Its states are **Starting**, **Provisioning**, **Ready**, **Paused** and **Ended**. |
| **Environment** | The machines or cluster behind a session. Its type shows on the scenario page, for example **Virtual Cluster**, **Lightweight Kubernetes**, **Multi Node Cluster** or **Virtual Machine**. |
| **Lifetime** | The maximum duration of a session, set by the scenario. Paused time is not counted. When it runs out, the session ends automatically. |
| **Pause** | Pausing a session stops its environment while keeping your work; you **Resume** it later at the same point. |
| **Catalog** | The list of scenarios your organization can launch, filterable by product, level, source and tag. |
| **Tutorial** | A guided, step-by-step lab where each step can be checked. Hints are free. |
| **Challenge** | A graded task on a real environment. Your work is graded when you submit; hints cost points. |
| **Playground** | A blank environment with no instructions and no grading, for open exploration. |
| **Assessment** | A timed, proctored technical assessment made of one or more scenarios, which a candidate takes by invitation. Also the type of scenario reserved for that use. |
| **Scenario Studio** | Klastro’s scenario authoring tool, available on the plans that include it. |

## Inside the lab

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Task** | A goal to reach in a scenario. In a tutorial, tasks show as **Steps**. |
| **Check** | The automatic test that inspects your environment to decide whether a task passes. In a tutorial you run it with **Check this step**. |
| **Hint** | Help you reveal when you need it. Free in a tutorial; costs points in a challenge or an assessment. |
| **Points** and **Score** | Each graded task is worth a number of points; the score is the total you earn. A **Pass mark** may apply. |
| **Session shell** | The lab’s main terminal, with `kubectl`, `helm` and `ssh` access to every machine. |
| **Notes** | A notepad saved automatically and attached to the session. In an assessment, the recruiter sees it in your report. |
| **Lobby** | An assessment’s waiting page, where your scenarios are prepared before you **Enter**. |

## Training

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Certification path** | A roadmap of steps from the first concept to exam readiness. Progress is self-declared: milestones, not credentials. |
| **Track** | An instructor’s reusable curriculum: an ordered sequence of scenarios plus its materials. Not to be confused with a certification path. |
| **Cohort** | One delivery of a track to a group, at a scheduled time, led by an instructor. |
| **Training session** | What a participant sees of a cohort in **My Training**. |
| **Trainee** (cohort) | A person enrolled in a cohort (a cohort participant). Not to be confused with the free Community seat holder, see **Trainee (seat)**. |
| **Place** | A trainee’s slot in a cohort, paid for in credits by the instructor. A place is not a seat. |

## Organization and billing

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Organization** | The shared space of a company, school or community: members, plan, credits, scenarios. You can belong to several organizations. |
| **Plan** | **Community**, **Pro**, **Instructor** or **Enterprise**. It decides which products and scenarios you can reach. |
| **Credits** | The unit labs are paid in, metered on real running time and weighted by environment. |
| **Trainee (seat)** | A member who holds the free Community seat. |
| **Seat** | The access level given to a member: **Trainee** (free, Community), **Pro Learner** or **Instructor**. |
| **Voucher** | A code that adds credits to your balance. Any member can redeem one. |
| **Invitation** | An emailed link to join Klastro, join an organization, or take an assessment. An invitation can expire or be revoked. |
