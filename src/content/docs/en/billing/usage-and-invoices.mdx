The **Billing** page shows what you have spent and why. What you see depends on
your role:

| Tab | Who sees it | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| **Overview** | Everyone | Your own credits. Admins and billing admins also see the plan summary and recent organization activity. |
| **Plan** | **Admin**, **Billing Admin** | The organization's subscription. |
| **Seats** | **Admin**, **Billing Admin** | The organization's seat pool. |
| **Usage** | Everyone | Your own balance and your own labs. |
| **History** | **Admin**, **Billing Admin** | Every credit movement in the organization. |

Credit figures always describe **your own** balance, even for an admin: each
member spends their own credits, and the organization holds none of its own.

:::note
Organizations under an Enterprise contract have no credits. Their **Billing**
page shows only the **Enterprise contract** notice and the **Contracted
capacity**. See [Plans and credits](/en/billing/plans-and-credits/).
:::

## Overview tab

The **Credits this period** card shows a meter of credits used out of your
grant, the credits **remaining**, and when they renew:

| Line | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Refreshes** followed by a date | Seat credits renew on that date. |
| **Refreshes monthly** | Seat credits renew every month. |
| **One-time grant, does not renew** | Your organization is on Community, whose credits are granted once. |
| **Concurrent sessions** · **up to** N | How many labs can run at once across your organization, when a limit is set. |

Admins and billing admins also see the plan summary (plan, **Billing** cadence,
**Seats**, **Credit grant**, and **Renews** or **Cancels** date) and a **Recent
activity** list of the latest credit movements.

## Usage tab

### This period

**Granted**, **Used** and **Remaining** show where your own balance stands right
now. **Granted** is what you had this period, spent or not, so a zero means you
had no credits.

### What a lab costs

A reference table of each **Environment** and its **Credits / hour**. See
[How a lab consumes credits](/en/billing/plans-and-credits/#how-a-lab-consumes-credits).

### Your labs

Every lab charged to you, so you can check a charge against a lab you remember
running. A lab appears here when it ends.

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Session** | The lab session. |
| **Started** / **Ended** | When the lab started and ended. |
| **Billed for** | The running time you were charged for. When the lab was paused, it reads, for example, **1h 10m less 20m paused**: paused time is free. |
| **Rate** | The environment's credits per hour, for example **2/h**. |
| **Credits** | What the lab cost, to the hundredth of a credit. |

If you have not run a lab yet, the table reads **No labs charged yet**.

## History tab

The **Credit ledger** lists every credit movement in the organization, most
recent first. Because each member has their own balance, the **Member** column
says whose balance moved, and **Balance** is that member's balance after the
movement.

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Type** | The kind of movement (see below). |
| **Member** | Whose balance changed. |
| **Reference** | What the movement relates to, such as a lab session. |
| **Date** | When it happened. |
| **Amount** | Credits added (positive) or spent (negative). |
| **Balance** | The member's balance afterwards. |

### Movement types

| Type | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Session usage** | A lab ended and its cost was charged. |
| **Plan credit grant** | A plan granted credits: the one-time Community welcome credits, or a paid seat's monthly renewal. |
| **Grant expired** | Unused credits were cleared just before a monthly renewal. Seat credits do not roll over. |
| **Cohort overage** | Cohort places beyond those included, paid in credits before launch. |
| **Credit transfer** | Credits given from one member to another. A transfer shows as two rows: one for the giver, one for the recipient. |
| **Seat assigned** | A paid seat was assigned to the member, with the credits left on it. |
| **Seat released** | A paid seat was taken back, with the credits left on it. |
| `VOUCHER_REDEMPTION` | A member redeemed a credit voucher. |

:::note
**Seat assigned** and **Seat released** rows do not create or destroy credits:
they show who may now spend the credits already on that seat.
:::

## Invoices and payment

Klastro charges your subscription through Stripe. There are no separate invoices
for lab usage: labs are paid in credits, and once your credits run out, labs
pause rather than run up an overage.

Invoices and receipts are sent by email to the **Billing email** set in your
organization settings. See
[Organization settings](/en/admins/organization-settings/#billing-information).

To update your payment method, change plan or cancel:

<Steps>

1. Open **Billing**, then the **Plan** tab.

2. Click **Manage subscription**.

3. Make your changes in the secure billing portal.

</Steps>

If the portal does not open, the page shows **Could not open the billing
portal**. Try again, and if it keeps failing,
[open a support ticket](/en/support/open-ticket/).
