Paid seats work like a pool. You **buy** seats into the pool, which changes your
subscription, then **assign** them to members, which does not. Releasing a seat
returns it to the pool for someone else. For what each seat gives, see
[Plans and credits](/en/billing/plans-and-credits/#seats).

## Before you start

- You need the **Admin** or **Billing Admin** role.
- Your organization needs a live Pro or Instructor subscription. Without one, the
  **Seats** tab reads **Subscribe to Pro or Instructor from the Plan tab to start
  buying seats.** See [Subscribe to a plan](/en/billing/subscribe/).
- Organizations under an Enterprise contract have no seat pool: every member
  reaches every scenario, and the **Seats** tab and the **Seat** column are not
  shown.

## Read the Seats tab

Open **Billing**, then the **Seats** tab. From top to bottom:

| Area | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Instructor seats** / **Pro Learner seats** | Seats assigned out of seats purchased for each tier, how many are unassigned, and the monthly credits each seat carries. |
| **Seat licences** | One card per purchased seat, with who holds it and the credits left on it. Free seats read **Unassigned**. |
| **Adjust your paid seats** | Buy or remove seats of one tier. |
| Members table | Each member who can hold a seat, with their **Role**, the **Credits left / seat** on the seat they hold, and a **Seat** control. An **Unallocated pool** row totals the seats nobody holds yet. |

:::note[Credits stay with the seat]
A seat's credits belong to the seat, not to the person. When a seat changes hands
during the month, the new holder gets whatever the previous holder left on it, and
the seat refills to its full grant at the next monthly reset. That is why two free
seats of the same tier can hold different amounts.
:::

## Buy seats

<Steps>

1. Under **Adjust your paid seats**, pick the tier: **Pro Learner** or
   **Instructor**.

2. Set the quantity with the minus and plus buttons, or type it.

3. Click **Buy**, check the **Buy … seats?** dialog, then click **Buy seats**.

</Steps>

The seats are added to your subscription and your bill increases. They land in
the pool, unassigned.

## Assign a seat to a member

A member can receive a seat once they have signed in at least once. Until then,
the **Seat** column reads **Awaiting sign-in**.

<Steps>

1. In the members table of the **Seats** tab, open the **Seat** control on the
   member's row.

2. Choose **Pro Learner** or **Instructor**. Each option shows the seat's price
   for reference; a tier with no free seat left is greyed out.

3. In the **Give … seat?** dialog, check the credits the member will be able to
   spend. If several free seats of that tier hold different amounts, pick the one
   to hand over; the fullest is selected by default.

4. Click **Assign seat**.

</Steps>

Assigning a seat never charges you: it uses a seat you already paid for.

:::tip
Admins can do the same from the **Seat** column of the **Team** page. There the
dialog is titled **Allocate … seat** and the button is **Allocate seat**. See
[Members and roles](/en/admins/members-roles/).
:::

Only some roles can hold a seat. **Billing Admin** and **Recruiter** members show
a dash in the **Seat** column, with the tooltip **This role does not hold a
seat.**

## Move a member to another tier

Choose the other tier in the member's **Seat** control. Moving from **Pro
Learner** to **Instructor** is one step, but it needs a free Instructor seat: the
two tiers are bought separately, so a Pro seat cannot pay for an Instructor one.

## Release a seat

To take a paid seat back, choose **Trainee** in the member's **Seat** control.

- On the **Seats** tab, the seat is released straight away.
- On the **Team** page, a **Release seat** dialog asks you to confirm first.

The member becomes a trainee: they keep their account and their membership, but
reach only free scenarios and the sessions an instructor assigns them. The seat
goes back to the pool with the credits left on it. Releasing does not cancel or
lower your subscription.

## Remove seats from your subscription

<Steps>

1. Release the seats of that tier that members still hold. You cannot remove
   seats that are still assigned.

2. Under **Adjust your paid seats**, pick the tier and quantity, then click
   **Remove**.

3. Check the **Remove … seats?** dialog, then click **Remove seats**.

</Steps>

:::caution
Removing your **last** paid seats cancels the subscription at the end of the
period. You keep your plan and your credits until then.
:::
