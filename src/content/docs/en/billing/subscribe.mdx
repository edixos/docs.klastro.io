This page shows how to move your organization from the free Community plan to
Pro or Instructor, and how to manage the subscription afterwards. For what each
plan includes, see [Plans and credits](/en/billing/plans-and-credits/).

## Before you start

- You need the **Admin** or **Billing Admin** role. Other members see only the
  **Overview** and **Usage** tabs of the **Billing** page.
- Your organization must be on a self-serve subscription. An organization under
  an Enterprise contract does not buy plans online.

## Subscribe

<Steps>

1. In the sidebar, open **Billing**, then the **Plan** tab.

2. Choose how you want to be invoiced with the **Monthly** / **Yearly** toggle.
   On **Yearly**, each paid card shows the cost of twelve monthly payments struck
   through, and how many months you save.

3. Compare the **Included** and **Limits** lists on each card, then click
   **Upgrade to Pro** or **Upgrade to Instructor** on the plan you want.

4. Check the **Subscribe to …?** dialog. It shows the **Price**, the number of
   **Seats** (**1 to start**) and the **Credits with the seat**. Click **Continue
   to checkout**.

   Nothing is charged until you finish checkout, and you can add or remove seats
   afterwards.

5. Pay on the secure Stripe checkout page.

6. You return to the **Billing** page with a **Payment received** banner. Your
   subscription is being activated: your plan and credits appear within a
   moment.

</Steps>

Your subscription starts with one seat. To give it to someone, or to buy more,
see [Manage seats](/en/billing/seats/).

:::note
If you leave the checkout page without paying, you return with a **Checkout
canceled** banner. No charge was made, and you can pick a plan again whenever you
are ready.
:::

## Add seats of another tier

An organization can hold Pro seats and Instructor seats at the same time; each
tier is billed as its own line on the same subscription. Once you have a live
subscription, the paid cards on the **Plan** tab show what you already hold
(**purchased**, **assigned**, and how many are **free**) and offer **Add a Pro
seat** or **Add a Instructor seat** instead of an upgrade button.

<Steps>

1. On the **Plan** tab, click **Add a Pro seat** or **Add a Instructor seat**.

2. Check the dialog. Nothing is charged today: the rest of the current period is
   prorated onto your next invoice, and the seat then renews at the price shown.

3. Click **Confirm purchase**.

</Steps>

To buy several seats at once, use the **Seats** tab. See
[Manage seats](/en/billing/seats/).

## Change your plan or payment method

Payment details, plan changes and cancellation are handled in the secure billing
portal.

<Steps>

1. On the **Plan** tab, click **Manage subscription**.

2. In the billing portal, change plan, update your payment method, or cancel.

</Steps>

:::tip[Moving members from Pro to Instructor]
Because the two tiers are separate pools, you can also switch people one at a
time: buy Instructor seats, move each member to an **Instructor** seat, then
remove the Pro seats you no longer use from the **Seats** tab.
:::

## Cancel your subscription

You can cancel in two ways:

- click **Manage subscription** on the **Plan** tab and cancel in the billing
  portal;
- remove your last paid seats from the **Seats** tab. This cancels the
  subscription at the end of the period.

Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period. Until then, the
**Overview** tab reads **Canceled. You keep Pro and your credits until** the end
date, and everything keeps working. You can resume the subscription at any time
before that date from **Manage subscription**.

Once the period has ended, the **Overview** tab says your subscription has ended,
and you can resubscribe at any time from the **Plan** tab.

## Subscription status

The plan summary on the **Overview** tab shows your subscription status:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Active** | The subscription is running normally. |
| **Pending** | The subscription is being set up. |
| **Past due** | A payment failed. Your plan keeps working for now; update your payment method from **Manage subscription**. |
| **Canceled** | The subscription has ended. |

## Ask for an Enterprise contract

For proctored hiring assessments, a fixed concurrent-lab capacity or contract
billing, use the **Enterprise** card at the bottom of the **Plan** tab.

<Steps>

1. Click **Contact sales**.

2. Optionally, tell the team what they should know (team size, timeline, what you
   need).

3. Click **Send request**. The card confirms the date of your request and the
   address the team will reply to.

</Steps>
