Klastro sells hands-on lab time. This page explains the model behind the
**Billing** page: what each plan gives you, what a credit is, and why two labs
of the same length can cost different amounts.

## Two ways an organization is billed

Every organization is billed in one of two ways, and the difference shapes
everything else on this page.

**Self-serve subscription.** You pick a plan on the **Plan** tab and pay online.
Labs are paid for in credits, which come with your seats or are given to you.
Everything below about credits applies to these organizations.

**Enterprise contract.** Price, capacity and term are agreed in a contract with
the Klastro team; nothing is bought online. A contract organization has no
credits at all: its **Billing** page shows an **Enterprise contract** notice and
its **Contracted capacity** (how many sessions can run at once), and nothing
else. Plans, seats, credit meters, vouchers and the credit ledger are hidden,
because none of them describes how a contract is billed. To change your quota or
ask a billing question, contact your account manager.

## Plans

A subscription organization is always on one of three plans. Community is free
and every new organization starts on it. Pro and Instructor are paid **per
seat**: you buy as many seats of each tier as you need, and an organization can
hold Pro seats and Instructor seats at the same time.

| | Community | Pro | Instructor |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price (excl. VAT) | Free | €25 / user / mo, or €250 / user / yr | €50 / user / mo, or €500 / user / yr |
| Credits | 10 welcome credits, once, to the founder | 60 credits per seat, every month | 100 credits per seat, every month |
| Labs running at once | 1 per member | Up to 2 per seat | Up to 5 per seat |
| Catalog | Free scenarios | Every Pro scenario, for each member holding a seat | Every Pro scenario |
| Scenario Studio | No | No | Yes, previews cost no credits |
| Instructor-led cohorts | No | No | Yes, 3 places included per cohort |
| Support | Community support, no response target | Business hours, 8-hour response target | Business hours, 4-hour response target |

The **Plan** tab always shows the current price and allowance for each tier,
alongside the **Included** and **Limits** lists for that tier.

:::note
Paying yearly changes how often you are invoiced, not how often you receive
credits. A yearly seat still receives its credit grant every month.
:::

### Rules on every plan

The **On every plan** box on the **Plan** tab lists the rules that apply
whatever you pay:

- Labs draw 1 to 6 credits an hour, depending on the environment.
- Starting a lab needs one hour of its credits in your balance.
- Labs pause when your credits run out. Paused time is not charged, and no
  overage is invoiced.
- One running lab per scenario for each member.
- Any member can redeem a credit voucher.

Your organization may also have a cap on how many labs run at once across the
whole organization. Paused labs do not count towards it.

## Seats

A **seat** is what a paid subscription is made of. Each member holds exactly one
seat type:

| Seat | What it gives | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Trainee** | The free floor every new member starts on. Reaches free scenarios and the sessions an instructor assigns. | Free, not part of your paid pool |
| **Pro Learner** | Every Pro scenario, plus the seat's monthly credits. | One Pro seat from your pool |
| **Instructor** | Everything Pro reaches, plus Scenario Studio, cohorts and the larger monthly allowance. | One Instructor seat from your pool |

Buying seats and giving them to people are two separate steps: you buy seats into
a **pool**, then an admin allocates them to members. Allocating or releasing a
seat never changes your bill. See [Manage seats](/en/billing/seats/).

## What a credit is

A credit is Klastro's unit of lab time. Every running lab spends credits from one
person's balance: the person who started it.

Your balance is personal. Each member has their own, and nobody else's spending
can drain it. It is made of:

- the credits on your seat, if you hold a paid seat. These reset to the seat's
  grant each month, unused credits do not roll over, and they stay with the seat:
  they cannot be given away;
- credits you hold yourself: the Community welcome credits, credits an admin gave
  you, and credits from a voucher you redeemed.

A member invited into an organization starts with no credits. Credits only
arrive explicitly: with a paid seat, as a gift from an admin, or from a voucher.

### Credits have decimals

Labs rarely run for exactly an hour, so credits are divisible to a hundredth.
Balances and charges show up to two decimal places, for example **0.14** or
**109.86**. A whole number of credits is shown without decimals.

## How a lab consumes credits

A lab is charged for the time it ran, weighted by how much compute its
environment uses. Heavier environments draw credits faster:

| Environment | Credits / hour |
|---|---|
| Virtual Cluster | 1 |
| Virtual Machine | 2 |
| Kubernetes in Docker | 3 |
| Lightweight Kubernetes | 3 |
| Multi Node Cluster | 6 |

The charge is the exact fraction of an hour the lab ran, rounded up to the next
hundredth of a credit. Four minutes on a 2 credits/hour environment costs
**0.14**, not 1. An hour on a Multi Node Cluster costs six times an hour on a
Virtual Cluster.

The charge is recorded when the lab ends. While a lab is running, the credits it
has used so far are already set aside from what you have left, so you cannot
start a second lab with credits the first one is spending.

### Starting and resuming a lab

To start a lab you need at least **one hour** of that environment's cost left in
your balance, after what your running labs have already used. A Multi Node
Cluster lab therefore needs 6 credits available; a Virtual Cluster lab needs 1.
Resuming a paused lab is held to the same rule.

### What happens when you run out

Klastro never bills you beyond your balance. If a long or heavy lab uses up your
credits while it is running, the lab is **paused automatically**. Your work is
kept, the paused time is free, and the audit log records **Session auto-paused
(out of credits)**. Once you have credits again (a new monthly grant, a gift from
an admin, or a voucher), you can resume the lab.

Only your own labs pause. Another member running out of credits never stops your
lab.

:::note
Labs that are part of an instructor-led cohort are paid for when the cohort is
launched. They are not charged again while they run and are never paused for
lack of credits. See [Run a cohort](/en/instructors/cohorts/).
:::

For help with a paused lab, see
[Credits and paused labs](/en/troubleshooting/credits-and-pauses/).

## Temporary access

Klastro can grant a member **temporary access** to a Pro or Instructor seat, for
example during an evaluation. It needs no subscription and ends on a fixed date.

Temporary access unlocks what the seat reaches, but it **includes no credits**:
labs still draw on the member's own balance. While it lasts, the member's
**Billing** page shows **Temporary Pro Learner access until** (or **Temporary
Instructor access until**) followed by the end date, and the **Team** page shows the seat
with **Temporary · until** and the date. When it ends, the organization's own
plan applies again.
