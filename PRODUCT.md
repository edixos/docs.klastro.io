# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People using app.klastro.io who are stuck, starting out, or looking something up: learners
practising on real clusters, candidates minutes before an assessment, recruiters reading a
report, instructors running a cohort live, admins handling seats and billing. Most arrive from
search or from a link in the app, read one page, and leave. Many read with a lab or the app open
in the next tab.

## Product Purpose

docs.klastro.io answers "how do I…" and "why did…" for app.klastro.io in French and English,
fast enough that the reader goes back to work. Success is a reader finding the right page from
search or the sidebar, following the steps without guessing, and never needing a support ticket.

## Brand Personality

Same voice as Klastro and Edixos: tech-minimal, precise, senior. Calm authority from people who
run production. Docs read like a well-kept internal runbook, not a marketing site.

## Anti-references

- Generic Starlight/Docusaurus default skin (purple accent, system font) that says "template".
- Rainbow callouts with thick coloured side stripes.
- Marketing decoration on reading pages: gradient text, glass cards, hero metrics, stagger reveals.
- Anything inherited from `ui/PRODUCT.md`'s anti-reference list.

## Design Principles

- **Same family as klastro.io and the app.** Identical fonts, palette and radii, so moving
  between marketing, docs and app feels like one product.
- **Reading first.** Measure, rhythm and contrast serve long how-to pages; nothing competes with
  the steps.
- **Restraint is the credibility signal.** One navy accent; semantic colour only for real state
  (tip, caution, danger).
- **Motion only as feedback.** Docs are a frequent-use tool: no entrance animations, only quick
  responsive feedback on hover and press.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast in light and dark themes, visible focus on every control, keyboard-navigable,
`prefers-reduced-motion` honoured, state never encoded in colour alone (asides carry icon + title).
